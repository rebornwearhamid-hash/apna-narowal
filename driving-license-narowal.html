Step by Step — New License

How to Apply for New Driving License from Narowal

  • Apply for Learner License online. Go to dlims.punjab.gov.pk, click "Learner License Application", enter your CNIC, fill the form and select vehicle category (motorcycle / car / other). Print the learner license application form.
  • Pay the learner license fee. Pay PKR 200 at any NBP bank branch or online via Punjab e-Pay using the challan number. Keep the payment receipt.
  • Get medical certificate. Visit a PMDC-registered doctor for a medical examination. The doctor certifies you are physically fit to drive. Fee: PKR 500–1,000 at any registered clinic.
  • Book appointment at DTO Gujranwala. Book online at dlims.punjab.gov.pk or call DTO Gujranwala (0552-823636). Select your date for biometrics and written test. DTO Gujranwala is on GT Road, Gujranwala — 60 km from Narowal (1.5 hours by bus).
  • Visit DTO Gujranwala on appointment date. Bring all original documents. Biometrics (fingerprints + photo) taken. Give written road safety test on computer — 20 multiple choice questions. Pass mark: 14/20.
  • Learner license issued. Learner license valid for 6 months. Practice driving under supervision of a licensed driver during this period.
  • Book practical driving test appointment. After minimum 6 weeks practice, book practical driving test appointment at DTO Gujranwala online or by phone.
  • Attend practical driving test at DTO. Drive the test vehicle through the test track — S-curve, hill start, parallel parking, emergency stop. Inspector assesses your driving.
  • Pay regular license fee. After passing practical test, pay PKR 700–3,000 depending on category and validity period (1 year or 5-year smart card).
  • Receive your driving license. Smart card license delivered within 15–30 days to your registered address via courier, or collect from DTO Gujranwala.

DTO Gujranwala — Getting There from Narowal

📍 District Transport Office (DTO) Gujranwala — GT Road, near Iqbal Stadium, Gujranwala. Phone: 0552-823636. Hours: Monday–Friday 8am–3pm.

🚌 From Narowal to Gujranwala: Take a bus from Narowal Bus Stand (0542-412900) to Gujranwala — PKR 150–250, takes 1.5 hours. Buses run every 30 minutes. Auto-rickshaw from Gujranwala Bus Stand to DTO: PKR 100–150.
⚠️ Important: Book your DTO appointment ONLINE at dlims.punjab.gov.pk before travelling to Gujranwala. Walk-in appointments are not always available. Travelling without an appointment may mean a wasted trip. Appointment slots open 2 weeks in advance.
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Where to apply for driving license from Narowal?▼
Narowal district residents apply at District Transport Office (DTO) Gujranwala on GT Road, Gujranwala (0552-823636). Apply online first at dlims.punjab.gov.pk then book appointment for biometrics and driving test at DTO Gujranwala — approximately 60 km from Narowal, 1.5 hours by bus.
How to check driving license status by CNIC?▼
Check license status at dlims.punjab.gov.pk by entering your 13-digit CNIC. You can also SMS your CNIC to 8585 for instant status — works without internet. The system shows license type, issue date, expiry date and status. Narowal CNICs start with 3460.
How to renew expired driving license in Narowal?▼
Apply for renewal online at dlims.punjab.gov.pk. If expired within 1 year: bring old license, CNIC, photos, medical certificate and pay renewal fee at NBP, then visit DTO Gujranwala for biometrics only (no test needed). If expired more than 1 year: you must retake the full driving test. Book DTO Gujranwala appointment online.
What is the driving test like at DTO Gujranwala?▼
The driving test has two parts: (1) Written test — 20 multiple choice questions on road safety, traffic signs and rules. Pass mark: 14/20. Done on computer at DTO. (2) Practical test — drive the test vehicle through S-curve, hill start, parallel parking and emergency stop on the DTO test track. An inspector assesses you during the practical test.
Can I get international driving license from Narowal?▼
International Driving Permit (IDP) is issued by the Pakistan Automobile Association (PAA) in Lahore. You need a valid Punjab driving license, CNIC, passport photos and pay the IDP fee of approximately PKR 2,000–3,000. Contact PAA Lahore for current procedures. IDP is valid for 1 year and recognised in most countries.
