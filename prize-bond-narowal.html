How to Check Prize Bond Result Online — Step by Step

  • Visit the official National Savings website: savings.gov.pk
  • Click on Prize Bond from the main menu
  • Select Check Result / Draw Lists
  • Choose your denomination (Rs 100, 750, 1500 etc)
  • Select the draw date/year you want to check
  • Enter your bond number and click Search
  • If your number appears — Congratulations! You have won
  • To claim go to any National Savings Centre with original bond and CNIC

How to Claim Prize Bond Winning in Narowal

If your prize bond number appears in the draw results, visit National Savings Centre on GT Road Narowal with your original prize bond and original CNIC. For prizes up to Rs 35,000 payment is made same day in cash. For higher amounts payment is via bank cheque or transfer and may take a few days.

⚠️ Important: Prize bond winnings must be claimed within 6 years of the draw date. After 6 years unclaimed prizes cannot be paid. Always check your bonds after every draw.
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How to check prize bond result 2026?▼
Use the checker tool at the top of this page or visit savings.gov.pk directly. Select your denomination, choose the draw date, enter your bond number and check. You can also visit National Savings Centre on GT Road Narowal to check in person.
Where to buy prize bonds in Narowal?▼
Buy at National Savings Centre GT Road Narowal (0542-412700), HBL Bank, NBP Bank, or MCB Bank Narowal. You can also buy online at savings.gov.pk. All banks issue prize bonds at face value — no commission or extra charge.
Can I sell my prize bond anytime?▼
Yes. Prize bonds are fully encashable at any time at face value. Visit any National Savings Centre or bank branch with your original bond and CNIC. You will receive the exact face value regardless of when you encash it.
Which prize bond is best to buy?▼
For most people in Narowal, Rs 750 and Rs 1,500 bonds offer good balance of affordability and prize amounts. The Rs 40,000 Premium bond offers the highest prizes (Rs 80 crore first prize) but requires larger investment. All bonds are equally safe — government guaranteed.
Is prize bond income taxable in Pakistan?▼
Yes. Prize bond winnings are subject to withholding tax in Pakistan. For filers the tax rate is 15% and for non-filers it is 25% on prize winnings. The tax is deducted at source when you claim your prize. File your income tax return to pay the lower 15% rate.
