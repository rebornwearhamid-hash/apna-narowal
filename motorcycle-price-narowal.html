How to Buy Motorcycle on Installments in Narowal

  • Choose your motorcycle model and visit the authorized dealer or bank in Narowal
  • Pay down payment — typically 20-30% of bike price
  • Submit CNIC, proof of income (salary slip or business proof) and 2 photos for financing application
  • Bank/dealer processes application — approval usually takes 1-3 days
  • Sign the installment agreement — choose 12, 24 or 36 month plans
  • Collect your motorcycle same day or within a few days after approval
  • Pay monthly installments via bank, easypaisa or dealer collection

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of Honda CD70 in Pakistan 2026?▼
Honda CD70 price in Pakistan 2026 is approximately PKR 195,000 to 225,000 depending on variant (Standard or Dream with self-start). Prices vary slightly by city and dealer. Check with your local Honda dealer in Narowal or Sialkot for the exact current price as prices change frequently.
Where to buy motorcycle in Narowal on installments?▼
Motorcycles can be purchased on installments in Narowal through bank leasing (HBL, MCB, UBL bike finance), authorized Honda/Yamaha/Suzuki dealer installment plans, or Easypaisa/JazzCash bike financing. Typical down payment is 20-30% with monthly installments over 12-36 months.
Which is the best 70cc bike in Pakistan 2026?▼
Honda CD70 remains the most popular 70cc bike due to reliability, fuel efficiency (65-70 km/L) and excellent resale value. Alternatives include Suzuki Sprinter 110 and budget brands like United and Road Prince at lower prices but with lower resale value.
How to verify motorcycle before buying used bike in Narowal?▼
Use our vehicle verification tool to check MTMIS registration, confirm owner details and check for any outstanding token tax. Verify the chassis and engine number physically match the registration book. Test ride the bike and check documents thoroughly before making payment.
