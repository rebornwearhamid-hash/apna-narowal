How to Check BISE Result Online — Step by Step

  • Go to official website bisegrw.com or use the checker above
  • Click on Results in the top menu
  • Select your exam type — Matric (SSC) or Intermediate (HSSC)
  • Select year 2025 and part (Part-I or Part-II)
  • Enter your Roll Number from your admit card
  • Click Submit — your result will display with all subject marks
  • Take a screenshot or print the result page for your records

What If You Fail? — Supply Exam Guide

If you fail in 1 or 2 subjects, you can appear in the Supply (Supplementary) examination conducted by BISE Gujranwala in October-November. Students who fail in more than 2 subjects must repeat the full year. Supply exam registration opens within 30 days of the main result announcement.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to check BISE Gujranwala result for Narowal?▼
Visit bisegrw.com, click Results, select your exam type and year, then enter your roll number. All Narowal district students (Narowal city, Shakargarh, Zafarwal) are under BISE Gujranwala. You can also use the checker tool at the top of this page.
When will matric result 2025 be announced?▼
BISE Gujranwala 10th class (matric) result 2025 is expected in July–August 2025. The 9th class result usually follows in August–September. Check bisegrw.com for the official announcement as exact dates are confirmed closer to the time.
What board is Narowal district under?▼
All schools and colleges in Narowal district including Narowal city, Shakargarh and Zafarwal are affiliated with BISE Gujranwala (Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education Gujranwala). Contact: 055-9200581. Website: bisegrw.com.
What is BISE Gujranwala contact number?▼
BISE Gujranwala contact number is 055-9200581. Address: Civil Lines, Gujranwala. Website: bisegrw.com. Office hours Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm. For result queries visit the website — phone lines are often busy during result season.
How to apply for rechecking of BISE result?▼
Apply for rechecking within 15 days of result announcement. Download the form from bisegrw.com, fill it, pay the fee at a designated bank, and submit at BISE Gujranwala office or through the online portal. Rechecking results come in 30–45 days.
What is the passing marks in BISE Gujranwala?▼
The minimum passing marks in BISE Gujranwala is 33% in each subject and 33% aggregate. Students must pass all subjects individually. Failing 1–2 subjects means supply (supplementary) exam. Failing 3 or more subjects means repeating the full year.
