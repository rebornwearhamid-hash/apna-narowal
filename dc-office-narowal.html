How to Get Domicile Certificate in Narowal

Visit DC Office at Civil Lines between 9am–5pm Mon–Fri. Go to Revenue section, request a domicile form. Attach CNIC copy, educational certificate or property document, and 2 passport photos. Submit and collect in 1–3 working days. Completely free.

Tip: Arrive before 10am to avoid queues. Thursdays are typically less busy.
