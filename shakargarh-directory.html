🏛️ Shakargarh (شکرگڑھ) is the capital of Shakargarh Tehsil, the largest of Narowal district's three tehsils by both area (835 km²) and population (769,339 as of the 2023 census). It sits in the north-east of Narowal district, close to the Ravi river and the international border with India, and carries a history distinct from the rest of the district.
History

The Story of Shakargarh

🐎 Why "Gateway of Mughals"? Shakargarh sat along the historic route Mughal emperors travelled between Lahore and Delhi via Gurdaspur — earning it this nickname over centuries as a waypoint for imperial caravans moving through Punjab.

Shakargarh's history is unlike anywhere else in Narowal district. Before the creation of Pakistan, it was Tehsil Shakargarh of Gurdaspur District in British India — administratively separate from what is now Narowal city. That changed dramatically at Partition.

1849
Sialkot annexed by the British following the Second Anglo-Sikh War, bringing the wider region under colonial administration.
1853
Shakargarh Tehsil (previously under Sialkot) is transferred to Gurdaspur District — a status that would last nearly a century.
1912
Shakargarh town is formally established and incorporated, becoming the administrative headquarters of the tehsil.
1947
The Radcliffe Award — under the boundary commission that partitioned Punjab, Gurdaspur District's four tehsils were split: three went to India, but Shakargarh alone was awarded to Pakistan, joining Sialkot District at independence. This makes Shakargarh's border-town status genuinely unique in the district's history.
1971
Shakargarh's proximity to the international border made it a key battleground during the Indo-Pak war, giving rise to its second nickname — "City of the Braves" — in recognition of local resilience during the conflict.
1991
Narowal District is newly created out of Sialkot District, combining the Narowal and Shakargarh tehsils. Shakargarh formally becomes part of Narowal district as of July 1, 1991.
2009
Zafarwal is carved out as the district's third tehsil, finalizing Narowal district's current three-tehsil structure: Narowal, Shakargarh and Zafarwal.

People & Culture

Shakargarh Tehsil is home to an overwhelming Muslim majority (around 99%), with small Christian, Sikh, Hindu and Ahmadi communities. The dominant caste group is Gujjar, followed by Ansari, Pathan, Jatt and Rajput communities — giving the tehsil a distinct social fabric compared to Narowal city. Local governance runs through Municipal Committee Shakargarh (the tehsil's only urban local government) alongside the wider Tehsil Council Shakargarh covering surrounding rural areas.

Geography — Where Shakargarh Sits

Shakargarh Tehsil covers 835 km², making it the largest of Narowal's three tehsils — larger than Narowal tehsil itself (1,065 km² total district-wide comparison) and significantly bigger than Zafarwal (437 km²). It borders the Ravi river to the east and lies close to the India-Pakistan international border, a legacy of its Gurdaspur-era geography. The tehsil is organized into 28 Union Councils.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Shakargarh called the Gateway of Mughals?▼
Shakargarh earned the nickname "Gateway of Mughals" because it historically lay on the route Mughal emperors used when travelling from Lahore towards Gurdaspur and onward to Delhi. It is also known as the "City of the Braves" for its role in the 1971 Indo-Pak war, when it was a key battleground close to the international border.
When did Shakargarh become part of Pakistan?▼
Before the 1947 Partition, Shakargarh Tehsil was part of Gurdaspur District, British India. Under the Radcliffe Award, Gurdaspur was mostly given to India, but Shakargarh was the only one of Gurdaspur's four tehsils awarded to Pakistan — making it a unique border tehsil that joined Sialkot District at independence.
When did Shakargarh become part of Narowal district?▼
Shakargarh was a tehsil of Sialkot District until 1991, when Narowal District was newly created out of the Narowal and Shakargarh tehsils of Sialkot. Since July 1, 1991, Shakargarh has been part of Narowal District as its largest tehsil by area and population.
What is the population of Shakargarh?▼
According to the 2023 census, Shakargarh Tehsil has a population of approximately 769,339, making it the largest tehsil in Narowal district by population. Shakargarh city itself has a population of around 126,742. The tehsil's literacy rate is 76.28%.
What is the majority community in Shakargarh?▼
Shakargarh Tehsil's population is overwhelmingly Muslim (around 99%), with small Christian, Sikh, Hindu and Ahmadi minorities. Gujjars are the predominant caste, followed by Ansaris, Pathans, Jatts and Rajputs.
