How Petrol Prices are Set in Pakistan

Pakistan petrol prices are revised every 15 days by OGRA (Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority) based on three main factors: international crude oil price (Brent crude), USD/PKR exchange rate, and government taxes (petroleum levy and GST). The Ministry of Finance approves the final prices which apply uniformly to all petrol stations across Pakistan including Narowal.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the petrol price in Narowal today?▼
Today petrol price in Narowal is shown in the live rate table above. Pakistan has a uniform petrol price across all cities set by OGRA every 15 days. The current RON 92 petrol rate, diesel (HSD) rate and other fuel prices are the same at all petrol stations in Narowal.
When does petrol price change in Pakistan?▼
Petrol prices in Pakistan change every 15 days — on the 1st and 15th of each month. OGRA reviews international oil prices and USD/PKR rate and announces new prices. The new rate is effective from midnight of the revision date.
Which petrol stations are in Narowal?▼
Narowal has PSO, Shell, Attock and Total Parco petrol stations on GT Road and main routes. Shakargarh and Zafarwal also have PSO and Hascol stations on GT Road. All stations charge the official OGRA national rate.
What is the difference between RON 92 and RON 95?▼
RON 92 is standard petrol suitable for most regular cars and motorcycles in Pakistan. RON 95 is premium petrol for modern high-performance and fuel-injected engines. RON 95 costs slightly more. For most bikes and regular cars in Narowal, RON 92 is the right choice.
Is the petrol price the same in Narowal and Lahore?▼
Yes. Petrol prices are uniform across all of Pakistan. The price you pay at a Narowal petrol pump is exactly the same as in Lahore, Karachi or Islamabad. No pump can legally charge above or below the official OGRA rate.
What is OGRA helpline number?▼
OGRA helpline is 0800-26726 (toll free). You can call to report overcharging at petrol stations, short filling (short measure), or to verify the official current fuel prices. OGRA website: ogra.org.pk.
