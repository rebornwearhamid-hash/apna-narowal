About UON

University of Narowal — Complete Guide

University of Narowal (UON) is a public sector university established in April 2018 through an act of the Punjab Provincial Assembly. Located on Shakargarh Road, Narowal, it serves students from Narowal, Shakargarh, Zafarwal, and surrounding areas including Sialkot district. UON offers quality higher education at affordable fees with over 20 departments across sciences, social sciences, commerce, and humanities.

How to Apply — UON Admissions 2025

  • Visit uon.edu.pk during the admission window (Fall: Aug–Sep / Spring: Jan–Feb)
  • Create an account and fill the online application form
  • Select your preferred program and mode (Morning or Self-Support)
  • Upload required documents: CNIC / B-Form, Matric certificate, Intermediate certificate, 2 passport photos
  • Pay the application fee via bank challan (amount shown on portal)
  • Appear for entry test if required for your program
  • Check merit list on uon.edu.pk — usually released within 2 weeks of deadline
  • If selected, deposit semester fee and complete enrollment at UON campus

UON Merit List 2025

University of Narowal merit lists are published on the official website uon.edu.pk. The Fall 2025 first merit list is expected in late September 2025. Merit is calculated based on intermediate percentage and entry test score. Students not on first merit list should watch for second and third merit lists.

How to Get to University of Narowal

UON is located on Shakargarh Road, Narowal city. From Narowal bus stand take any wagon or rickshaw heading towards Shakargarh — the university gate is approximately 2 km from the city centre. From Sialkot take a direct wagon to Narowal (45 min) then local transport to UON.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the contact number of University of Narowal?▼
University of Narowal contact number is 0542-411600. Address: Shakargarh Road, Narowal, Punjab 51600. Official website: uon.edu.pk. You can also email through the website contact form.
When does UON admission open in 2025?▼
University of Narowal Fall 2025 admissions typically open in August with a deadline in September. Spring admissions open in January. Always check uon.edu.pk for exact dates as they are announced officially each semester.
What is the fee structure of University of Narowal?▼
BS morning program fees are approximately PKR 15,000–22,000 per semester depending on the department. Self-support programs are higher at PKR 22,000–35,000 per semester. MS/MPhil fees range from PKR 25,000–55,000 per semester. Always check uon.edu.pk for the latest official fee challan.
Is University of Narowal HEC recognised?▼
Yes. University of Narowal is a government university established by Punjab Provincial Assembly Act and is fully recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan. Degrees from UON are valid for government jobs and further studies.
Can students from Sialkot apply to University of Narowal?▼
Yes. University of Narowal is open to all Pakistani students regardless of district. Many students from Sialkot, Gujranwala, and other nearby districts study at UON. Hostel facilities are available on campus for outstation students.
Does University of Narowal have a hostel?▼
Yes. University of Narowal has separate hostel facilities for male and female students on campus. Hostel seats are limited — apply early through the admissions office. Contact 0542-411600 for hostel availability and fee details.
