Services Available at BHU/Dispensary

  • Free outpatient consultation with Medical Officer
  • Basic medicines provided free of charge
  • Child immunization/vaccination (EPI program)
  • Maternal health services — antenatal checkups
  • First aid for minor injuries
  • Family planning counseling and services
  • Referral to DHQ/THQ Hospital for serious cases
  • Basic lab tests (at select larger BHUs)

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What services are available at BHU in Narowal?▼
Basic Health Units (BHU) in Narowal district provide free outpatient consultation, basic medicines, vaccination/immunization for children, maternal health services, first aid and referral to DHQ Hospital for serious cases. All services are completely free of charge for residents.
Are dispensary services free in Narowal?▼
Yes. Government dispensaries and Basic Health Units (BHU) in Narowal district provide completely free consultation and basic medicines to all residents. No fee is charged for outpatient services at these government health facilities.
How to find nearest dispensary in Narowal district?▼
Government dispensaries and BHUs are located in most union councils across Narowal, Shakargarh and Zafarwal tehsils. Ask your local union council office or call DHQ Hospital Narowal (0542-412345) for information on the nearest facility to your village or area.
