Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is DHQ Hospital Narowal contact number?▼
DHQ Hospital Narowal contact number is 0542-412345. Emergency department open 24 hours, 7 days a week including weekends and public holidays.
Is DHQ Hospital Narowal free?▼
Yes. DHQ Hospital Narowal provides completely free healthcare — OPD, emergency, surgery, maternity, medicines, and lab tests are all free for all patients.
What departments are in DHQ Hospital Narowal?▼
DHQ Hospital Narowal has general medicine, surgery, paediatrics, gynaecology, orthopaedics, ophthalmology, ENT, dermatology, emergency, blood bank, lab, radiology, pharmacy, and ICU.
Where is DHQ Hospital Narowal located?▼
DHQ Hospital is on Hospital Road, Narowal City, Punjab. Easily accessible from the city centre by rickshaw or taxi. Parking available.
