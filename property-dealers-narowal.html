How to Buy Property in Narowal — Step by Step

  • Contact a trusted property dealer like UPA Umar Property Advisor (0304-6213850) and tell them your requirements and budget
  • Visit shortlisted properties personally — never buy without visiting
  • Verify the Fard (ownership record) at the Narowal Patwari office — confirm the seller is the legal owner
  • Check for any loans, mortgages or disputes on the property at DC Office Narowal
  • Agree on the price and get a written agreement (Bayana) with witnesses
  • Complete the registry (transfer of ownership) at the Sub-Registrar Office, DC Office Narowal
  • Pay stamp duty and registration fees at the designated bank
  • Collect your registered Deed — the property is now legally yours
