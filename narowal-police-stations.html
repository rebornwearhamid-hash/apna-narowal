How to Register FIR in Narowal

  • Visit the police station covering the area where the incident occurred, with your original CNIC
  • Report the incident to the duty officer (Munshi) — explain what happened, when, where and any witnesses
  • The duty officer records your complaint in the FIR register — you have the legal right to have your FIR registered for cognizable offences
  • Get a copy of the FIR — this is your right and proof of registration. FIR number is important for tracking
  • For certain offences (mobile theft, vehicle theft, lost CNIC/passport) you can register e-FIR online at punjabpolice.gov.pk without visiting the station
  • If police refuse to register your FIR, you can approach the DPO office (0542-412050) or file a complaint with a magistrate under Section 22-A/22-B CrPC

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Police helpline number in Punjab?▼
Punjab Police emergency helpline is 15, available 24/7 across Punjab including Narowal district. Rescue 1122 handles medical emergencies and fire. DPO Narowal office direct line is 0542-412050 for non-emergency matters. Highway Patrol is 130 for road accidents on GT Road.
How to register FIR in Narowal?▼
Visit your nearest police station with your original CNIC and details of the incident. The duty officer records your complaint in the FIR register. You have a legal right to have your FIR registered for cognizable offences. Get a copy with the FIR number. Certain offences can also be registered online via e-FIR at punjabpolice.gov.pk.
Where is DPO office in Narowal?▼
District Police Officer (DPO) office Narowal is on Circular Road, Civil Lines, Narowal — near the District Court. Contact: 0542-412050. The DPO office handles district-wide police administration, serious crime coordination and complaints against police officers.
How to file a complaint against police in Narowal?▼
Contact the DPO office directly at 0542-412050 with a written complaint and any evidence. You can also file online at the Punjab Police Complaint Cell via punjabpolice.gov.pk. For serious matters you can approach the Provincial Police Officer complaint cell in Lahore.
Is there a women's police desk in Narowal?▼
Yes. A Women Police Desk is available at Narowal City Police Station (0542-412051) with female officers to handle complaints about harassment, domestic violence and gender-based crimes in a supportive environment. This ensures women feel comfortable reporting such incidents.
