Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the load shedding schedule in Narowal today?▼
The load shedding schedule for Narowal today is shown in the tables above. MEPCO rotates load shedding across feeders throughout the day. Urban Narowal city typically gets 6-8 hours in summer. Call MEPCO helpline 118 (free) for the latest official schedule.
What is MEPCO complaint number for Narowal?▼
MEPCO helpline for Narowal is 118 — free call available 24 hours. You can also call 0800-6372 (toll free) or visit MEPCO office on GT Road Narowal for complaints about load shedding, low voltage, or electricity faults.
How many hours is load shedding in Narowal?▼
In summer (June–August) urban Narowal typically gets 6–8 hours of load shedding per day. Rural feeders in Narowal, Shakargarh and Zafarwal can get 10–14 hours. In winter (November–February) it reduces to 2–4 hours daily. Scheduled and unscheduled outages are separate.
Which company manages electricity in Narowal?▼
MEPCO — Multan Electric Power Company — manages electricity distribution in Narowal district. MEPCO is a subsidiary of WAPDA. Their Narowal office is on GT Road and their helpline is 118 (free, 24 hours).
Is rural Narowal load shedding different from city?▼
Yes. Urban feeders (Narowal city centre) get less load shedding than rural feeders. Rural areas in Narowal, Shakargarh and Zafarwal tehsils can experience 10–14 hours of load shedding daily in summer compared to 6–8 hours in urban areas.
What is the best solution for load shedding in Narowal?▼
Most Narowal households use a UPS with batteries for short outages. For longer term, solar panel systems are the most cost-effective solution. Small 3-5 panel solar systems can power basic home needs during load shedding hours. Local solar dealers are available in Main Bazaar Narowal.
