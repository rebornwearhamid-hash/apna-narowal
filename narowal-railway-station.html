Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Narowal Railway Station contact number?▼
Narowal Railway Station: 0542-410011. National Pakistan Railways helpline (free): 117 — available daily.
What time does the train leave Narowal for Lahore?▼
Narowal Express departs at approximately 6:30 AM, arriving Lahore around 9:45 AM. Confirm current timings by calling 0542-410011.
How long is Narowal to Lahore by train?▼
The train journey from Narowal to Lahore takes approximately 3 to 3.5 hours depending on service and stops.
How do I book a train ticket from Narowal?▼
Buy at Narowal Railway Station counter (Railway Road) up to 10 days in advance, or online at pr.gov.pk. Pay via cash, debit card, or JazzCash.
Is there a railway station in Shakargarh?▼
Yes. Shakargarh Railway Station on Station Road (0544-500300) connects to Narowal and Lahore via Pakistan Railways.
