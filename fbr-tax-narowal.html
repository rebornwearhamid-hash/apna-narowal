Filer vs Non-Filer

Why Become a Tax Filer

✅ FILER Benefits
  • Lower withholding tax on bank withdrawal
  • Lower tax on property purchase/sale
  • Lower tax on vehicle registration
  • Can claim tax refunds
  • Easier loan/mortgage approval
  • Eligible for govt tenders/contracts
❌ NON-FILER Drawbacks
  • Double withholding tax on many transactions
  • Higher tax on property transactions
  • Higher tax on vehicle purchase
  • Cannot claim tax refunds
  • Difficulty getting bank loans
  • Not eligible for govt contracts

How to Register NTN Online — Step by Step

  • Visit iris.fbr.gov.pk and click "Registration for Unregistered Person"
  • Enter your CNIC number, mobile number and email address
  • FBR sends verification codes via SMS and email — enter both to verify
  • Complete the registration form with your address (Narowal district) and source of income (salary, business, etc)
  • Your NTN (National Tax Number) is generated instantly — same as your CNIC for individuals
  • Create your Iris login credentials to file tax returns going forward
  • You are now registered — file your annual income tax return to appear on the Active Taxpayer List (ATL) and become a filer

How to File Income Tax Return

  • Log in to Iris portal (iris.fbr.gov.pk) with your registered credentials
  • Select the relevant tax year and choose the appropriate return form (salaried, business, AOP, etc)
  • Enter your income details — salary, business income, property income, other sources
  • Declare your assets — property, vehicles, bank balances, investments
  • Enter tax deducted at source (from salary slip, bank statements, etc)
  • Review and submit your return before the deadline (typically September 30 for individuals)
  • Once processed, you appear on the Active Taxpayer List (ATL) as a filer
⚠️ Important: The tax return deadline is typically September 30 each year (may be extended). Late filing results in penalties and you lose filer status for that period. Keep proper records of your income, bank statements and any assets purchased/sold during the year.
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How to register NTN online in Pakistan?▼
Register NTN at iris.fbr.gov.pk. Click Registration for Unregistered Person, enter your CNIC, mobile number and email. FBR sends verification codes via SMS and email. Complete the form with your address and income source. NTN is issued instantly and is the same as your CNIC for individuals.
How to check filer status in Pakistan?▼
Check filer status at fbr.gov.pk by searching the Active Taxpayer List (ATL) with your CNIC. You can also SMS your CNIC to 9966 for instant status. Being a filer means you filed your income tax return for the current tax year and appear on the ATL.
What are the benefits of being a tax filer in Pakistan?▼
Filers pay significantly lower withholding tax on bank transactions, property purchase, vehicle registration and other transactions compared to non-filers. Filers can also claim tax refunds, get easier loan approvals, and are eligible for government tenders. Non-filers pay much higher withholding tax rates on the same transactions.
What is the income tax slab in Pakistan 2026?▼
For salaried individuals: no tax up to PKR 600,000 annual income, then progressive rates from 5% to 35% for higher brackets. Rates are revised annually in the federal budget — always verify exact current slabs at fbr.gov.pk before filing your return.
Do I need to file tax return if my income is below taxable limit?▼
You are not legally required to file if your income is below the taxable threshold, but filing anyway makes you a filer and gives you the reduced withholding tax benefits on transactions like property purchase, vehicle registration and banking. Many people file a nil/zero return specifically to gain filer status benefits.
