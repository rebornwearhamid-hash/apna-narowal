📜 Narowal district falls under BISE Gujranwala (Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education Gujranwala) for matric (9th/10th) and intermediate examinations. All schools in Narowal, Shakargarh and Zafarwal are registered with BISE Gujranwala.

How to Check Matric Result Online

  • Visit bisegrw.edu.pk — official BISE Gujranwala website
  • Click on "Result" section and select Matric/SSC Result
  • Choose the year (2026) and enter your roll number
  • Click Submit — your result appears with subject-wise marks, total marks and grade
  • Alternatively, SMS BISEGRW [Roll No] to 8300 for instant result via text message
  • Take a screenshot or print of your online result for immediate use (original DMC comes from your school)

How to Get Your Official Result Card (DMC)

The online result is provisional. Your official DMC (Detailed Marks Certificate) is sent by BISE Gujranwala to your school. Visit your school in Narowal after results are announced (usually 2-4 weeks later) to collect your original DMC. This is required for college admissions.

After Matric — Next Steps for Narowal Students

After receiving your matric result, apply for admission to Narowal colleges for intermediate (FSc/ICS/ICOM/FA). Pre-Medical and Pre-Engineering programs are competitive and merit-based. Check our Colleges in Narowal guide for admission details and program options.

⚠️ If not satisfied with result: You can apply for rechecking/rescrutiny within 15 days of result announcement at BISE Gujranwala office. There is a small fee per subject for rechecking. Supplementary exams are also held for students who did not pass in certain subjects.
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How to check matric result for Narowal 2026?▼
Check matric result at bisegrw.edu.pk by entering your roll number. Narowal falls under BISE Gujranwala board. You can also SMS "BISEGRW [Roll Number]" to 8300 for instant result. Results are typically announced in June (9th) and July (10th) for annual exams.
Which board does Narowal fall under for matric?▼
Narowal district falls under BISE Gujranwala (Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education Gujranwala) for matric (9th and 10th class) and intermediate examinations. All schools in Narowal, Shakargarh and Zafarwal tehsils are registered with BISE Gujranwala.
How to get matric result card/certificate from Narowal?▼
After checking your provisional result online, visit your school in Narowal to collect your original DMC (Detailed Marks Certificate) which BISE Gujranwala sends to schools 2-4 weeks after results. For duplicate or attested certificates, visit BISE Gujranwala office directly.
What to do if failed in matric from Narowal?▼
If you failed one or more subjects, you are eligible for supplementary exams typically held a few months after the annual result. Check bisegrw.edu.pk for supplementary exam schedule and registration. You can also apply for rechecking if you believe there was a marking error, within 15 days of result announcement.
