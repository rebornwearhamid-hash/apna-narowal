How to Apply for Government Jobs in Narowal

  • Regularly check PPSC website (ppsc.gop.pk) for Punjab government job advertisements specific to Narowal district cadre
  • Check NTS website (nts.org.pk) for testing-based recruitment (teaching, health, various departments)
  • Read local newspapers (Jang, Nawa-i-Waqt, Nation) for job advertisements — many government jobs are still primarily advertised in print
  • Prepare required documents: CNIC, educational certificates (attested), domicile certificate, photos
  • Apply online before the deadline — pay the test/application fee through the specified bank challan
  • Prepare for the written test (MCQs on general knowledge, subject knowledge, English) if applicable
  • Attend interview if shortlisted — bring all original documents

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How to apply for government jobs in Narowal?▼
Government jobs in Narowal are advertised through PPSC (ppsc.gop.pk), NTS (nts.org.pk), or directly by the hiring department. Check newspapers and official websites regularly. Apply online before the deadline with required documents (CNIC, attested certificates, domicile) and the test fee via bank challan.
What jobs are available in Narowal district?▼
Narowal offers jobs in government sector (education, health, police, revenue), textile and garment manufacturing (RebornWear and other sublimation factories), banking, teaching in private and government schools/UON, agriculture-related businesses and retail/trade. Sialkot's nearby sports and surgical industry also employs many Narowal residents.
How to find private jobs in Narowal?▼
Find private jobs through local newspaper classifieds, Facebook job groups for Narowal, direct applications to local businesses and factories (especially textile/sportswear), and online platforms like Rozee.pk. Networking through local business contacts and visiting factories directly for walk-in interviews is also effective.
Are there teaching jobs at University of Narowal?▼
Yes. University of Narowal (UON) regularly advertises faculty positions across departments including Engineering, Computer Science, Business, English and Islamic Studies. Check uon.edu.pk career/jobs section for current openings. Minimum requirement is typically MPhil, with PhD preferred for senior positions.
What is the salary range for factory jobs in Narowal?▼
Factory jobs in Narowal's textile/sportswear sector vary by role — production workers typically earn PKR 25,000-40,000/month, skilled tailors PKR 30,000-50,000/month, and supervisors/QC inspectors PKR 40,000-70,000/month depending on experience. Rates vary by factory and are often piece-rate for production roles.
