Who Can Donate Blood — Eligibility

  • Age between 18 and 60 years
  • Weight at least 50 kg (110 lbs)
  • Hemoglobin level above 12.5 g/dL
  • No fever or active infection in the past 2 weeks
  • No major surgery in the past 6 months
  • Not donated blood within the last 3 months (12 weeks)
  • No history of Hepatitis B, C or HIV
  • Not pregnant or breastfeeding (for women)
  • Had a proper meal before donating — not on empty stomach

How to Donate Blood in Narowal — Step by Step

  • Visit DHQ Hospital Blood Bank Narowal or THQ Shakargarh with your original CNIC
  • Fill a short donor screening form about your health history and travel history
  • Get a quick hemoglobin test (finger prick) to confirm you meet the minimum level
  • Blood pressure and pulse check by medical staff
  • Donation takes only 10-15 minutes — approximately 350-450ml of blood is drawn
  • Rest for 10-15 minutes with refreshments provided (juice, biscuits) before leaving
  • You will receive a donor card — keep it for tracking your donation history and 3-month eligibility
⚠️ Thalassemia Awareness: Narowal has thalassemia patients who need blood transfusions every 2-4 weeks for life. If you know a regular donor with a compatible blood group, connecting them with these patients through DHQ Hospital can make a huge difference. Consider getting a thalassemia carrier screening before marriage.
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I get blood urgently in Narowal?▼
For urgent blood needs visit DHQ Hospital Blood Bank Narowal (0542-412345) which operates 24 hours. You can also contact THQ Shakargarh Hospital blood bank (0544-500500) or reach out to local blood donor WhatsApp groups for rare blood groups. In serious emergencies call Rescue 1122.
Who can donate blood in Pakistan?▼
You must be 18-60 years old, weigh at least 50kg, have hemoglobin above 12.5 g/dL, and be in good general health. You cannot donate if you have fever, recent surgery within 6 months, certain infections like Hepatitis or HIV, or donated blood within the last 3 months.
How often can you donate blood?▼
Healthy adults can donate whole blood every 3 months (12 weeks). This gives your body enough time to replenish red blood cells and iron stores. Regular donation every 3 months is completely safe and is encouraged for eligible donors to help maintain blood supply.
What is the blood group compatibility chart?▼
O-negative is the universal donor and can be given to all blood types in emergencies. AB-positive is the universal recipient and can receive from all groups. Use the interactive checker above to see full compatibility for your specific blood group.
Does blood donation hurt or have side effects?▼
Blood donation involves minor discomfort from the needle prick, similar to a blood test. The process takes 10-15 minutes and is very safe. Some people feel slightly dizzy afterward — resting 10-15 minutes with refreshments before leaving prevents this. Your body replenishes the donated blood volume within 24-48 hours.
