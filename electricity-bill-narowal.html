How to Check MEPCO Bill Online — Step by Step

  • Go to mepco.com.pk
  • Click on Bill Inquiry from the main menu
  • Enter your 14-digit Reference Number from your old bill
  • Click Submit — your current bill details appear
  • Note the amount due and last date for payment
  • Click Print Bill to get a duplicate bill for payment at bank
  • Pay via JazzCash, EasyPaisa, bank branch, or online banking

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How to check MEPCO electricity bill online in Narowal?▼
Use the bill checker at the top of this page or visit mepco.com.pk directly. Click Bill Inquiry and enter your 14-digit reference number from your previous bill. Your current bill amount, due date, and units consumed will appear instantly.
What is MEPCO helpline for Narowal?▼
MEPCO helpline for Narowal is 118 — free call available 24 hours a day. For billing complaints call during office hours for faster resolution. You can also visit MEPCO office on GT Road Narowal Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm.
How to pay electricity bill via JazzCash in Narowal?▼
Open JazzCash app, tap Pay Bills, select MEPCO from the list, enter your 14-digit reference number, verify the amount, and confirm payment with your PIN. Payment is instant and you receive an SMS confirmation. No charges for MEPCO bill payment.
Where is the reference number on MEPCO bill?▼
Your MEPCO reference number is a 14-digit number printed on the top right corner of your electricity bill, usually labeled as Ref No. or Reference Number. Keep it saved on your phone for easy online bill checking anytime.
How to get duplicate MEPCO bill in Narowal?▼
Visit mepco.com.pk, go to Bill Inquiry, enter your 14-digit reference number, and click Print Bill to download a duplicate bill PDF. You can also get a duplicate at the MEPCO office GT Road Narowal during office hours.
My MEPCO bill is too high — what to do?▼
Call MEPCO helpline 118 immediately to file a high bill complaint. They will send a meter reader within 3 working days. If your meter is faulty, MEPCO will replace it and adjust the bill. You can also file a complaint at mepco.com.pk online portal.
