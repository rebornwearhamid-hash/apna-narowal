Is Property a Good Investment in Narowal?

Property in Narowal has seen steady appreciation over the past 3–5 years. The Ring Road project and improved motorway connectivity to Lahore have made GT Road properties particularly valuable. Civil Lines and College Road are also growing in demand. Rural land near major roads is attracting agricultural investors.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the property rate per marla in Narowal 2025?▼
Residential land on GT Road Narowal costs approximately PKR 18–25 lakh per marla in 2025. Civil Lines is 15–20 lakh, College Road is 12–18 lakh, and outskirts are 4–8 lakh per marla.
Are property prices increasing in Narowal?▼
Yes. Property prices in Narowal have been rising due to the Ring Road project and improved Lahore motorway connectivity. GT Road and Civil Lines areas have seen 15–20% increases over recent years.
What is the property rate in Shakargarh per marla?▼
Residential property in Shakargarh city costs approximately PKR 6–12 lakh per marla in 2025. Commercial rates on main roads are PKR 12–25 lakh per marla.
How much does a 5 marla house cost in Narowal?▼
A 5 marla house in Narowal city costs approximately PKR 60 to 120 lakh depending on location, condition, and proximity to main roads. GT Road area houses are priced higher.
