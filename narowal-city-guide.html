About Narowal — Punjab, Pakistan

Narowal district is located in the northeastern corner of Punjab province, sharing its eastern border with India across the Ravi river. Established as a separate district in 1991, it is divided into three tehsils: Narowal, Shakargarh, and Zafarwal.

Getting to Narowal

Narowal is well connected by road and rail. By road it is 120 km from Lahore via the M-11 motorway. Regular buses and wagons run from Lahore (Thokar Niaz Baig), Sialkot, and Gujranwala. Narowal Railway Station connects to Lahore and Sialkot via Pakistan Railways. See our railway station guide for train timings.

Economy of Narowal

The economy is predominantly agricultural — rice, wheat, sugarcane, and vegetables. Small industries include textiles, agricultural tools, and food processing. The main bazaar is a major commercial hub for the sub-region.

Healthcare in Narowal

DHQ Hospital Narowal is the primary free government hospital. Three THQ hospitals serve Shakargarh and Zafarwal. Private hospitals including Al Rehman Hospital and Medicare Hospital provide additional specialist care. See our best hospitals guide.

Education in Narowal

Government Boys and Girls High Schools provide free matric education. Private schools including Beacon House and The Educators offer Cambridge programs. See our schools guide.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Narowal located?▼
Narowal is in northeastern Punjab, Pakistan, approximately 120 km from Lahore and 45 km from Sialkot. It borders India to the east along the Ravi river.
What is Narowal famous for?▼
Narowal is famous for agricultural production (rice, wheat, sugarcane), historic Sufi shrines, and its location near the India-Pakistan border. It is known for the warm hospitality of its people.
How many people live in Narowal?▼
Narowal district has a population of approximately 1.8 million people spread across three tehsils: Narowal, Shakargarh, and Zafarwal.
How do I get from Lahore to Narowal?▼
From Lahore take the M-11 motorway towards Gujranwala then GT Road to Narowal — approximately 120 km. Bus services run from Thokar Niaz Baig. Train service available from Lahore to Narowal Railway Station.
