PEEF — Punjab Educational Endowment Fund
Full/Partial
✅ Covers: Tuition fees + stipend for deserving students
Need-based scholarship for Punjab students including Narowal at matric, intermediate and undergraduate level. Priority to orphans and financially deserving students. Apply online with academic records and income certificate.
Apply at peef.org.pk →
HEC Need-Based Scholarship
Pakistan
✅ Covers: Tuition + partial living costs
Higher Education Commission offers need-based scholarships for undergraduate students at recognized Pakistani universities. Based on financial need and academic merit. Narowal students studying at any HEC-recognized university can apply.
Apply at hec.gov.pk →
Ehsaas Undergraduate Scholarship
Full
✅ Covers: Full tuition + stipend
Federal government scholarship for financially deserving undergraduate students across Pakistan including Narowal district. Covers full tuition and living stipend for the entire degree program.
Apply at hec.gov.pk →

Education stipend for children of BISP Kafaalat families to keep them enrolled in school. Available for Narowal families already registered in BISP. See our BISP Narowal guide for full details.

CSC — Chinese Government Scholarship
Full
✅ Covers: Tuition + accommodation + monthly stipend + insurance
One of the most generous scholarships available to Pakistani students. Covers full tuition, dormitory accommodation, monthly stipend CNY 2,500-3,500, and health insurance. Apply via Chinese Embassy Islamabad or HEC.

Turkiye Burslari — Turkey Scholarship
Full
✅ Covers: Tuition + accommodation + monthly stipend + flight
Turkey's premier full scholarship for international students. Covers full tuition, government dormitory, monthly stipend TRY 5,000-7,000, health insurance and one return flight ticket. Deadline January-February each year.
See Turkey Visa Guide →
Chevening Scholarship (UK)
Full (Master's)
✅ Covers: Full tuition + living costs for Master's in UK
Prestigious UK government scholarship for Master's study, fully funded. Highly competitive — requires strong academics, leadership experience and clear career goals. Apply at chevening.org between August-November.

Full/Partial
✅ Covers: Monthly stipend + tuition (if applicable) + travel
German Academic Exchange Service offers various scholarships for Master's and PhD study in Germany. Combined with Germany's free public university tuition, this significantly reduces study costs. Apply at daad.de.

Full

Australian government scholarship for Master's and PhD study, fully funded including living allowance and airfare. Highly competitive with focus on development-related fields. Apply through Australian High Commission.

🎓 Scholarships significantly reduce or eliminate education costs for deserving students. Narowal students should explore both Pakistan-based options (PEEF, HEC, Ehsaas) for local studies and full study-abroad scholarships (China, Turkey especially) for international education.

How to Apply for Scholarships — General Guide

  • Maintain strong academic record — most scholarships require good grades (70%+ typically)
  • Prepare required documents: academic transcripts, CNIC/B-Form, domicile, income certificate (for need-based)
  • Write a strong motivation letter/SOP explaining your goals and why you deserve the scholarship
  • Get recommendation letters from teachers or professors where required
  • Apply well before deadlines — many scholarships have limited annual application windows
  • For study-abroad scholarships, also prepare IELTS/language test scores as required
  • Follow up and track your application status through the respective portal

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PEEF scholarship and who can apply?▼
PEEF (Punjab Educational Endowment Fund) provides need-based scholarships to Punjab students including Narowal district for matric, intermediate and undergraduate studies. Apply online at peef.org.pk with academic records and income certificate. Priority is given to orphans and financially deserving students.
What HEC scholarships are available for Narowal students?▼
HEC offers need-based scholarships, Ehsaas Undergraduate Scholarship, indigenous PhD scholarships, and various international scholarships for study abroad. Apply at hec.gov.pk. Narowal students studying at any HEC-recognized Pakistani university are eligible to apply.
Which countries offer the best scholarships for Pakistani students?▼
China (CSC scholarship) and Turkey (Turkiye Burslari) offer generous full scholarships covering tuition, accommodation and stipend with relatively achievable requirements. UK, Canada, USA and Australia offer prestigious but highly competitive fully-funded scholarships (Chevening, Australia Awards) that are harder to secure.
