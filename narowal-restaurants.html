Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best restaurants in Narowal?▼
Top restaurants: Al Baik (GT Road, best karahi/BBQ), Lala Ji Tikka (Truck Adda Road, best late-night BBQ), Royal Biryani (Station Road), Punjab Darbar (cheapest desi food).
Are there family restaurants in Narowal?▼
Yes. Al Baik Restaurant on GT Road and Family Restaurant Shakargarh are both popular family choices with spacious seating and desi food.
What is the cheapest restaurant in Narowal?▼
Punjab Darbar on Main Bazaar. Full desi meal (daal, sabzi, chapatti, lassi) for PKR 150–200.
Which restaurants are open late in Narowal?▼
Lala Ji Tikka House is open until 2am. Al Baik Restaurant until midnight. Truck Adda dhabas are open 24 hours.
Are there restaurants in Shakargarh?▼
Yes. Family Restaurant Shakargarh (0544-504200) on Main Chowk is popular for desi food and biryani.
