Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best schools in Narowal?▼
Best private: The Educators Narowal (Cambridge + matric) and Beacon House (O/A Level). Best free: Govt Boys High School and Govt Girls High School Narowal.
Are government schools free in Narowal?▼
Yes. All government schools in Narowal including Govt Boys and Girls High Schools are completely free under the Punjab Education Department.
What is the fee at private schools in Narowal?▼
Monthly fees range from PKR 1,500 (Oxford Academy) to PKR 8,000 (Beacon House) depending on school and program.
Are there Cambridge schools in Narowal?▼
Yes. Beacon House Narowal offers O Level and A Level. The Educators Narowal offers Cambridge from primary level. Both are on Main Boulevard and GT Road respectively.
When do school admissions open in Narowal?▼
Government school admissions open in March–April. Private school admissions are open year-round. Contact schools directly for current availability.
