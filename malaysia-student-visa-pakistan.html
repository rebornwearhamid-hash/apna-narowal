🇲🇾 Malaysia is an excellent study destination for Pakistani students — it is a Muslim-majority country with halal food, affordable English-medium education, and a welcoming environment. Unique to Malaysia: your university handles the Student Pass application through EMGS (Education Malaysia Global Services) — you do not need to go to the embassy. The entire process is online.

Step by Step

How to Apply for Malaysia Student Pass from Pakistan

  • Choose a Malaysian university. Search for programs at educationmalaysia.gov.my or directly on university websites. Top choices for Pakistani students: Universiti Malaya (UM), Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM), Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM), International Islamic University Malaysia (IIUM — very popular with Pakistanis), HELP University, Taylor's University, Asia Pacific University (APU).
  • Apply for admission to the university. Submit online application with FSc/A-Level certificates, IELTS score, academic transcripts, SOP and any other required documents. Many Malaysian universities have rolling admissions with intakes in January, July and September.
  • Receive your offer letter. After acceptance the university sends an offer/acceptance letter. Pay the required deposit to confirm your place.
  • University submits EMGS application on your behalf. This is unique to Malaysia — you do not apply for the visa yourself. Your university submits your Student Pass application to EMGS (Education Malaysia Global Services) online. You only need to provide documents to your university. EMGS processes the application and communicates directly with the university.
  • EMGS approval — Visa Approval Letter (VAL). After EMGS approves your application (4–8 weeks), they issue a Visa Approval Letter (VAL). Your university forwards the VAL to you.
  • Get Single Entry Visa (SEV) from Malaysian High Commission Islamabad. With your VAL, apply for a Single Entry Visa at the Malaysian High Commission, Islamabad (Diplomatic Enclave). This is the only step requiring an embassy visit. Processing: 3–5 business days. Fee: approximately PKR 3,000–5,000.
  • Travel to Malaysia. Fly to Kuala Lumpur (direct flights from Lahore on AirAsia, Malaysia Airlines). Show your Single Entry Visa and Offer Letter at immigration.
  • Student Pass stamped in Malaysia. On arrival your university's international office takes you to the Immigration Department of Malaysia. Your Student Pass is stamped in your passport — valid for the duration of your course.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How does EMGS work for Malaysia student visa from Pakistan?▼
EMGS (Education Malaysia Global Services) is the official body that processes Student Pass applications for Malaysian universities. Your university submits your application to EMGS on your behalf — you just give documents to the university. EMGS processes in 4–8 weeks and issues a Visa Approval Letter (VAL). You then take the VAL to the Malaysian High Commission Islamabad to get a Single Entry Visa before traveling.
Is IIUM good for Pakistani students in Malaysia?▼
IIUM (International Islamic University Malaysia) is extremely popular among Pakistani students. It offers programs in Islamic Studies, Law, Engineering, Medicine, Economics and more in an Islamic environment. The campus has a large Pakistani student community, halal food, mosque on campus, and a welcoming atmosphere. IIUM has been educating Pakistani students for decades and offers affordable fees for a quality education.
What IELTS score is needed for Malaysia student visa?▼
Most Malaysian universities require IELTS 5.5 for undergraduate and 6.0 for postgraduate. Some universities like UM and UTM may require 6.0–6.5 for competitive programs. Malaysia is one of the more flexible destinations — some universities accept English medium school certificates instead of IELTS. Check individual university requirements.
How much does it cost to live in Kuala Lumpur as a Pakistani student?▼
Living costs in Kuala Lumpur are approximately MYR 1,500–2,500 per month (PKR 90,000–150,000) covering accommodation, food, transport and personal expenses. University dormitories are cheaper at MYR 500–800/month. Malaysian ringgit is favorable for Pakistani students. Total annual cost including tuition is roughly PKR 20–40 lakh — very affordable compared to UK or Australia.
Can I work in Malaysia on a student pass from Pakistan?▼
Yes but with restrictions. Malaysia Student Pass allows part-time work of up to 20 hours per week during semester breaks (not during term time). You need permission from the Immigration Department of Malaysia. On-campus student jobs (research assistant, teaching assistant) are generally permitted. Many Pakistani students supplement income through tutoring and freelancing online.
