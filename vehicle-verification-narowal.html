How It Works

How to Verify Vehicle in Narowal

  • Use the checker above — enter the registration number (e.g. NAW-123) and click Verify, OR visit mtmis.punjab.gov.pk directly
  • The system shows: owner name, father's name, CNIC (partial), vehicle make/model, engine number, chassis number, registration date, token tax status
  • Check the transfer history — how many times the vehicle changed hands and when
  • Check for any FIR or stolen vehicle status via Punjab Police: punjabpolice.gov.pk
  • For SMS verification: send registration number to 8785 — works without internet
  • For in-person verification: visit Excise Office Narowal (near DC Office, Civil Lines) — phone: 0542-412300

New Vehicle Registration in Narowal

  • Get Form A (vehicle registration application) from Excise Office Narowal or download at mtmis.punjab.gov.pk
  • Submit original CNIC, vehicle invoice/purchase receipt, fitness certificate and insurance certificate
  • Pay the registration fee at NBP bank — fee varies by vehicle type and engine size
  • Excise office issues temporary registration certificate immediately
  • Permanent number plate and registration book (log book) issued within 30–60 days

Vehicle Transfer (Ownership Change) in Narowal

  • Both buyer and seller visit Excise Office Narowal together with original CNICs
  • Seller signs the transfer form in front of the Excise officer
  • Buyer pays transfer fee — typically PKR 2,000–10,000 depending on vehicle value
  • Pay any outstanding token tax dues before transfer can proceed
  • New ownership is reflected in MTMIS database within 3–7 working days
  • Updated registration book issued in buyer's name
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How to verify vehicle registration in Narowal?▼
Use the verification tool above, visit mtmis.punjab.gov.pk, or SMS the registration number to 8785. For in-person verification visit Excise Office Narowal (Civil Lines, near DC Office) at 0542-412300. The system shows owner name, vehicle details, tax status and transfer history.
What is the registration code for Narowal vehicles?▼
Narowal city vehicles use the code NAW (e.g. NAW-1234). Shakargarh uses SGH and Zafarwal uses ZFW. All three are registered at the Excise and Taxation Office, Civil Lines, Narowal (0542-412300).
How to pay token tax online in Punjab?▼
Pay Punjab token tax online at epaytax.punjab.gov.pk or via Jazz Cash and Easypaisa apps using your vehicle registration number. You can also pay at any NBP branch in Narowal. Token tax is due annually — penalties apply for late payment at PKR 50–2,000 per month depending on vehicle type.
How to check if a vehicle is stolen in Punjab?▼
Check Punjab Police stolen vehicle database at punjabpolice.gov.pk or call 15. Also verify through MTMIS mtmis.punjab.gov.pk. If buying a used vehicle always check both systems. Any FIR or encumbrance on the vehicle will show in MTMIS and prevent registration transfer.
Where is the Excise office in Narowal?▼
Excise and Taxation Office Narowal is near the DC Office, Civil Lines, Narowal. Phone: 0542-412300. Office hours Monday to Friday 9am–5pm. Services: new registration, transfer, token tax payment, duplicate log book, fitness certificate.
