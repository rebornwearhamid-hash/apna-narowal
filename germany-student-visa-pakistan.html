🎓 FREE Tuition at German Public Universities
Pakistani students pay ZERO tuition fees at most German public universities. Only a semester contribution of EUR 150–350 applies. Germany is the most affordable top-quality study destination in the world.
🇩🇪 Germany has 3 unique requirements that differ from UK/Canada/Australia: (1) APS Certificate — academic evaluation by German Embassy Islamabad, (2) Blocked Account — EUR 11,208 deposited in a German bank account, and (3) German language — required for most undergraduate programs (English-taught Master's also available).
Step by Step

How to Apply for Germany Student Visa from Pakistan

  • Learn German (if required). Most undergraduate programs are taught in German. You need at least B2 level German (TestDaF or DSH test). Many Master's programs are in English — search on daad.de. If you plan for German programs, start learning 1–2 years in advance. Goethe Institut Pakistan offers German language courses in Lahore and Karachi.
  • Get your APS Certificate — mandatory for Pakistanis. Visit APS Pakistan office at the German Embassy, Islamabad. Submit your academic certificates (Matric, FSc/A-Levels, Degree). Attend an APS interview. Fee: PKR 18,000. Processing: 4–6 weeks. Without APS you cannot apply to any German university. Book early — slots fill up.
  • Apply to German universities. Apply directly through the university website or via uni-assist.de (central application portal for many German universities). Use DAAD (daad.de) to search English-taught programs and scholarships. Popular choices: TU Munich, RWTH Aachen, University of Stuttgart, TU Berlin, University of Hamburg, Hochschule universities.
  • Receive your admission letter (Zulassung). After acceptance, you receive a Zulassung (admission letter) from the German university. This is required for your visa application.
  • Open a blocked account (Sperrkonto). Deposit EUR 11,208 (2026 requirement) into a German blocked account. Approved providers: Fintiba (fintiba.com) or Coracle (coracle.de). Account opens online in 1–3 days. The money is released monthly at EUR 934. Keep the blocked account confirmation for your visa.
  • Get health insurance. You need German public health insurance. Sign up with TK, AOK or Barmer from Pakistan (they have international enrollment). Cost: approximately EUR 110/month. Required for visa application.
  • Book appointment at German Embassy Islamabad. Go to pakistan.diplo.de and book a student visa appointment. German Embassy is in Islamabad — from Narowal take a bus to Lahore then to Islamabad (4–5 hours total).
  • Attend visa appointment at German Embassy Islamabad. Bring all original documents. Pay visa fee of EUR 75 (approximately PKR 24,000). The visa officer may ask about your study plans.
  • Wait for visa decision. Processing takes 6–12 weeks. Germany student visa processing from Pakistan can be slow — apply well in advance. You will receive a national visa (Type D) valid for 3 months to enter Germany.
  • Arrive in Germany and register. On arrival register at the local Einwohnermeldeamt (residents registration office) within 2 weeks. Enroll at your university and convert your entry visa to a residence permit (Aufenthaltserlaubnis) at the local Ausländerbehörde (foreigners office).

APS Certificate — Mandatory for Pakistani Students

⚠️ APS (Academic Evaluation Centre) is MANDATORY for all Pakistani students applying to German universities.
  • 📍 Location: German Embassy, Diplomatic Enclave, Islamabad
  • 💰 Fee: PKR 18,000 (approximately)
  • ⏱️ Processing time: 4–6 weeks
  • 📋 Submit: Matric, FSc/A-Levels, Degree certificates + transcripts
  • 🗣️ Interview: Academic interview in German or English
  • 🌐 Book at: aps-pakistan.de or German Embassy Islamabad
Get APS done 6+ months before your intended start date — it is the first step.

Blocked Account — EUR 11,208 Explained

🏦 A blocked account (Sperrkonto) is a special German bank account where you deposit EUR 11,208 to prove you can fund your living expenses for one year. The funds are released monthly at EUR 934. Open online at Fintiba (fintiba.com) or Coracle (coracle.de) before applying for the visa. Setup fee is approximately EUR 199. The EUR 11,208 is YOUR money — it just proves you have sufficient funds.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is education really free in Germany for Pakistani students?▼
Yes. Most German public universities charge no tuition fees for international students including Pakistanis. You only pay a semester contribution of EUR 150–350 per semester which covers public transport passes and student services. Private universities do charge fees but most top German universities are public and free. Total cost of living in Germany is EUR 800–1,200 per month.
What is the APS certificate and where to get it in Pakistan?▼
APS (Academic Evaluation Centre) certificate verifies your Pakistani academic credentials for German universities. Get it at the APS Pakistan office at the German Embassy, Diplomatic Enclave, Islamabad. Submit Matric, FSc and Degree certificates, pay PKR 18,000 and attend an academic interview. Processing takes 4–6 weeks. Book appointment at aps-pakistan.de.
Do I need to know German to study in Germany?▼
For undergraduate programs: yes, most are in German — you need B2 level (TestDaF or DSH test). For Master's programs: many are taught entirely in English — search on daad.de for English-taught programs. Engineering, IT, Business and Data Science have many English-taught options. If you plan to study undergraduate start German at Goethe Institut Lahore 1–2 years in advance.
How much money do I need for Germany student visa from Pakistan?▼
You need EUR 11,208 in a blocked account (your money — returned monthly at EUR 934). Plus APS fee PKR 18,000, visa fee EUR 75, health insurance EUR 110/month, blocked account setup EUR 199, and semester contribution EUR 150–350 per semester. Total initial outlay approximately EUR 12,000 (PKR 39 lakh) — but most of this is your own money returned to you.
Can I work in Germany on a student visa from Pakistan?▼
Yes. Germany Student Visa allows 120 full days or 240 half days of work per year (approximately 20 hours per week). German minimum wage is EUR 12.41/hour. After graduation you get an 18-month job-seeker visa to find work in Germany, and can then apply for a work permit and eventually permanent residence.
