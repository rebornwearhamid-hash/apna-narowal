🇨🇳 China is the most popular study destination for Pakistani students after UK and Canada. Pakistan and China share the CPEC relationship and China offers generous scholarships. The CSC (Chinese Government Scholarship) covers full tuition, accommodation, monthly stipend of CNY 2,500–3,500, and health insurance. Thousands of Narowal and Punjab students study in China each year.
Scholarships First

China Scholarships for Pakistani Students 2026

🏆
CSC — Chinese Government Scholarship
✅ Covers: Tuition + Accommodation + Stipend + Insurance
Full scholarship from Chinese Ministry of Education. Monthly stipend: CNY 2,500 (Bachelor's), CNY 3,000 (Master's), CNY 3,500 (PhD). Apply via Chinese Embassy Islamabad or HEC Pakistan. Deadline: March–April each year for September intake.
🎓
HEC — Pakistan-China Scholarship
✅ Covers: Tuition + Stipend (partial/full)
Higher Education Commission Pakistan allocates China scholarships annually. Apply at hec.gov.pk. Strong academics required (70%+ marks). HEC also has specific CPEC scholarships for engineering and technical fields for Pakistani students.
🏛️
University-Direct Scholarships
✅ Covers: Partial tuition reduction
Many Chinese universities offer their own scholarships to Pakistani students — 50-100% tuition waiver. Apply directly to the university. Easier to get than CSC. Check university websites for scholarship portals.
🌉
CPEC Scholarship
✅ Covers: Tuition + Living
Special CPEC (China-Pakistan Economic Corridor) scholarships for engineering, IT, and technical fields. Priority for students from Punjab, KPK and Balochistan. Apply via HEC Pakistan and provincial higher education departments.
Step by Step

How to Apply for China Student Visa from Pakistan

  • Choose your path — Scholarship or Self-funded. First decide: CSC Scholarship (apply Jan–April via Chinese Embassy or HEC) or direct admission (apply to university directly). Scholarship path takes longer but covers all costs. Direct admission is faster but you pay fees yourself.
  • Apply for CSC Scholarship (if applying for scholarship). Create account at csc.edu.cn. Fill the online application, upload academic transcripts, IELTS/HSK certificate, motivation letter, two recommendation letters, and study plan. Submit through Chinese Embassy Islamabad OR through HEC Pakistan portal. Deadline: typically March 31 each year.
  • Apply directly to Chinese university (if self-funded). Go to the university website or use CUCAS (cucas.cn — popular for international students). Submit online application with academic documents, IELTS score (for English programs) or HSK score (for Chinese programs). Most universities have rolling admissions.
  • Receive Admission Notice and JW201/JW202 form. After acceptance the university sends: (1) Admission Notice and (2) JW201 or JW202 form — these two documents are mandatory for your visa application. JW201 is for scholarship students, JW202 for self-funded.
  • Complete Physical Examination. Download the Foreigner Physical Examination Form from the Chinese Embassy website. Get a medical exam at a government hospital or approved clinic in Pakistan. This is required for the visa and for your residence permit in China.
  • Get a police clearance certificate. Obtain a No Criminal Record Certificate from the Pakistan police. Required for visa application. Get it from your local NADRA or through police verification portal.
  • Apply for X1 visa at Chinese Embassy or Consulate. Apply at Chinese Embassy Islamabad, Chinese Consulate Karachi, or Chinese Consulate Lahore (nearest for Narowal students). Submit all documents and pay the visa fee. Processing takes 2–4 weeks.
  • Arrive in China and register. On arrival register at the local Public Security Bureau (PSB) within 24 hours of arrival. Convert your X1 visa to a Residence Permit at the PSB within 30 days. Your university international office will guide you through this process.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How to apply for CSC scholarship from Pakistan?▼
Apply for CSC (Chinese Government Scholarship) through two channels: (1) Chinese Embassy Islamabad — go to pk.china-embassy.gov.cn, (2) HEC Pakistan — apply at hec.gov.pk. Create an account at csc.edu.cn, fill the online form, upload documents including transcripts, IELTS/HSK, motivation letter, study plan, and two recommendation letters. Deadline is typically March 31 for September intake.
What is the X1 visa for China from Pakistan?▼
X1 visa is the long-term student visa for China for students studying more than 180 days. It is converted to a Residence Permit after arriving in China within 30 days. Apply at Chinese Embassy Islamabad or Chinese Consulate Lahore or Karachi. You need your Admission Notice and JW201/JW202 form from the university. Processing takes 2–4 weeks.
What is the JW201 and JW202 form for China visa?▼
JW201 is the visa application form for CSC scholarship students. JW202 is for self-funded international students. Both are issued by the Chinese university and are mandatory for the X1 visa application. The university sends these forms along with your Admission Notice after you are accepted.
Is Chinese language required to study in China from Pakistan?▼
Not necessarily. Many Chinese universities offer full degree programs in English — especially Engineering, IT, Business, and Medicine at Master's and PhD level. For Chinese-taught programs you need HSK Level 4 or higher. For English-taught programs IELTS 5.5–6.0 or TOEFL is required. Check individual university requirements at cucas.cn or the university website.
Where to apply for China student visa near Narowal?▼
Apply at Chinese Consulate General Lahore (nearest to Narowal) or Chinese Embassy Islamabad. Chinese Consulate Lahore is on Ferozepur Road, Lahore — approximately 2.5 hours from Narowal. Book appointment online at the consulate website. You can also use a visa agent in Lahore to submit on your behalf.
