✍️ Faiz Ahmed Faiz (فیض احمد فیض) was born on February 13, 1911 in Narowal, Punjab, Pakistan (then British India). He is one of the most celebrated Urdu poets in history and Narowal's most famous son. His poetry is studied in schools and universities across Pakistan, India, and worldwide.
Biography

Life of Faiz Ahmed Faiz

Faiz Ahmed Faiz was born into a middle-class family in Narowal in 1911. His father, Sultan Muhammad Khan, was a lawyer who had worked with a British government official. Faiz showed exceptional academic ability from childhood and went on to study at Government College Lahore where he earned degrees in English and Arabic literature.

Faiz worked as a professor of English at Amritsar College before joining the British Indian Army during World War II, eventually rising to the rank of Lieutenant Colonel. After independence in 1947 he became editor of the influential Pakistani newspaper Pakistan Times.

His left-wing political views led to his arrest in the famous Rawalpindi Conspiracy Case in 1951, when he was imprisoned for nearly four years. It was during his imprisonment that he wrote some of his most powerful poetry — including the famous Zindan Nama (Prison Notebooks). He faced further persecution under the military regime of Ayub Khan and spent years in exile in Beirut where he edited the cultural magazine Lotus.

Despite government persecution Faiz's poetry gained worldwide recognition. He received the Lenin Peace Prize in 1962, was nominated for the Nobel Prize in Literature, and received Pakistan's highest civilian honour Nishan-e-Imtiaz. He died in Lahore on November 20, 1984 but his poetry lives on.

Famous Poetry by Faiz Ahmed Faiz

ہم دیکھیں گے (Hum Dekhenge)
ہم دیکھیں گے
لازم ہے کہ ہم بھی دیکھیں گے
وہ دن کہ جس کا وعدہ ہے
"We shall witness — it is certain that we too shall witness the day that has been promised to us."
From Mere Dil Mere Musafir (1981)
بول (Bol)
بول کہ لب آزاد ہیں تیرے
بول زبان اب تک تیری ہے
تیرا ستواں جسم ہے تیرا
"Speak, for your lips are free — speak, your tongue is still yours, this upright body is yours."
From Naqsh-e-Faryadi (1941)
مجھ سے پہلی سی محبت (Mujh Se Pehli Si)
مجھ سے پہلی سی محبت مرے محبوب نہ مانگ
میں نے سمجھا تھا کہ تو ہے تو درخشاں ہے حیات
"Do not ask me for that love again, my beloved — I had thought that you were everything, that life was radiant because of you."
From Naqsh-e-Faryadi (1941)
دوا کر (Dua Kar)
آئیے ہاتھ اٹھائیں ہم بھی

"Come, let us also raise our hands — we who do not know the custom of prayer."
Zindan Nama (Prison Notebooks)

Served in the British Indian Army, rising to the rank of Lieutenant Colonel. Awarded MBE for his military service.

After partition became editor of Pakistan Times, one of Pakistan's leading English newspapers. Actively involved in progressive politics.

Imprisoned — Rawalpindi Conspiracy Case
Arrested in the Rawalpindi Conspiracy Case — accused of planning a communist coup. Spent nearly four years in prison writing Zindan Nama (Prison Notebooks), some of his greatest poetry.

Awarded the prestigious Lenin Peace Prize by the Soviet Union, bringing international recognition. One of the few Asians to receive this honour.

Lived in Beirut editing the Afro-Asian Writers Association magazine Lotus during Zulfikar Ali Bhutto and Zia-ul-Haq eras. Witnessed the Lebanese Civil War.

Received Pakistan's highest civilian honour Nishan-e-Imtiaz. Died on November 20, 1984 in Lahore at age 73. Buried in Lahore. His death was mourned across Pakistan, India, and the world.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Where was Faiz Ahmed Faiz born?▼
Faiz Ahmed Faiz was born on February 13, 1911 in Narowal, Punjab, Pakistan (then British India). Narowal is proudly known as the birthplace of this legendary poet and is sometimes called the "City of Faiz." His birthplace in Narowal is a point of great pride for the district.
What is Faiz Ahmed Faiz famous for?▼
Faiz is famous for his revolutionary Urdu poetry that powerfully blended romantic love with political consciousness. His most famous poems include Hum Dekhenge, Bol, Mujh Se Pehli Si Muhabbat, and Subh-e-Azadi. He won the Lenin Peace Prize in 1962 and received multiple Nobel Prize nominations.
When and where did Faiz Ahmed Faiz die?▼
Faiz Ahmed Faiz died on November 20, 1984 in Lahore, Pakistan at the age of 73. He had returned to Pakistan after years of exile in Beirut. He is buried in Lahore. His death was mourned across Pakistan, India, and wherever Urdu poetry is loved.
What is the Faiz International Festival?▼
The Faiz International Festival is an annual cultural festival held in Lahore in honour of Faiz Ahmed Faiz. It features music, poetry readings, art exhibitions and cultural performances bringing together artists from across Pakistan and the world. It is one of Pakistan's largest cultural events.
Is there a museum or memorial for Faiz in Narowal?▼
Efforts have been made to establish a memorial and museum in Narowal to honour Faiz Ahmed Faiz's birthplace. The Faiz Foundation based in Lahore works to preserve and promote his literary legacy. Visitors to Narowal can ask local residents about the area where he was born in the old city.
