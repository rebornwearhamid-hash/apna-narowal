🇺🇸 USA F-1 Student Visa is for full-time academic study at a SEVP-approved US university or college. Unlike UK/Australia/Canada, the USA requires a face-to-face visa interview at the US Embassy Islamabad or US Consulate Lahore. The key unique steps are: getting an I-20 form from the university, paying the SEVIS fee of USD 350, and attending the interview.
Step by Step

How to Apply for USA F-1 Student Visa from Pakistan

  • Apply to a SEVP-approved US university. The university must be approved by SEVP (Student and Exchange Visitor Program). Apply through Common App (commonapp.org) for undergraduate or directly to the university for graduate. Popular choices: University of Houston, Texas A&M, Florida International University, George Mason University, University of Central Missouri, New York Institute of Technology (NYIT).
  • Receive your I-20 form. After accepting the offer and paying your deposit, the university's international student office sends you the I-20 (Certificate of Eligibility for Nonimmigrant Student Status). This is a mandatory document for F-1 visa.
  • Pay the SEVIS fee — USD 350. Go to fmjfee.com and pay the I-901 SEVIS fee of USD 350. Save your SEVIS fee receipt — you need it for the visa interview. SEVIS ID is printed on your I-20.
  • Complete the DS-160 online form. Go to ceac.state.gov and complete the DS-160 (Online Nonimmigrant Visa Application). Upload your photo. Save the confirmation barcode page — you need it for the appointment.
  • Pay the visa application fee — USD 185 (MRV fee). Pay the MRV fee at designated banks in Pakistan (HBL, MCB) or online. Keep the payment receipt.
  • Book visa interview appointment. Go to ustraveldocs.com/pk and schedule your visa interview at US Embassy Islamabad or US Consulate Lahore (nearest for Narowal students — 2.5 hours by bus). Interview slots can be busy — book early.
  • Prepare your financial documents. Show bank statements covering the full cost of attendance — tuition + living expenses (typically USD 25,000–65,000 per year depending on university). Show 6 months of bank statements for yourself and/or sponsor (parents).
  • Attend the visa interview. Go to the US Consulate Lahore or Embassy Islamabad on your appointment date with all documents. The visa officer will ask 5–10 questions. Be confident, honest and concise. Answer in English.
  • Wait for visa processing. If approved, your passport will be returned with the F-1 visa stamp within 2–5 business days via courier. If additional processing (221g) is required it may take 2–8 weeks.
  • Travel to USA. You can enter the USA up to 30 days before your course start date. Show your F-1 visa, I-20, and proof of funds at US port of entry.

Visa Interview Tips — US Consulate Lahore / Embassy Islamabad

✅ Do This
→ Know your I-20 details (course, university, start date)
→ Show strong ties to Pakistan (family, property, career plan)
→ Explain clearly why you chose this university/course
→ Be confident and speak in English
→ Bring original documents — organized in order
→ Dress professionally — shirt/shalwar kameez OK
→ Answer briefly and to the point
❌ Avoid This
→ Saying you want to stay in USA permanently
→ Being vague about why you chose this university
→ Bringing too many unnecessary documents
→ Showing no ties to Pakistan
→ Giving rehearsed or robotic answers
→ Being nervous or hesitant
→ Mentioning relatives who are US citizens

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the I-20 form for USA student visa?▼
The I-20 is the Certificate of Eligibility for Nonimmigrant Student Status issued by your US university's international student office. It contains your SEVIS ID number, course details, cost of attendance, and start/end dates. You need I-20 to pay the SEVIS fee and for your visa interview. Keep it safe — you will need it throughout your studies in the USA.
What is the SEVIS fee for USA student visa from Pakistan?▼
The SEVIS fee for F-1 Student Visa is USD 350 (approximately PKR 97,000). Pay online at fmjfee.com using your SEVIS ID from the I-20. Pay at least 3 days before your visa interview. Keep the I-901 SEVIS fee receipt — you must show it at the interview.
How much bank balance is needed for USA student visa from Pakistan?▼
You need to show bank statements covering the full cost of attendance shown on your I-20 — this includes tuition plus living expenses. Typically USD 25,000–65,000 per year depending on the university. Show 6 months of bank statements for you and/or your parents as sponsors. Liquid funds (bank balance) are most convincing.
Where to attend USA visa interview near Narowal?▼
The nearest visa interview location for Narowal students is the US Consulate General in Lahore (Shimla Hill, Lahore). Lahore is approximately 2.5 hours from Narowal by bus. You can also attend at the US Embassy in Islamabad. Book your appointment at ustraveldocs.com/pk. Arrive 15 minutes early with all original documents.
Can I work in USA on F-1 student visa?▼
On F-1 visa you can work on-campus up to 20 hours per week during term time. Off-campus work is restricted during studies but you get Optional Practical Training (OPT) after graduation — 12 months of work authorization in your field, extendable to 36 months for STEM graduates. OPT is a major pathway to H-1B work visa and US Green Card.
