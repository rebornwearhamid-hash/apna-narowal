Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest food in Narowal?▼
Punjab Darbar on Main Bazaar is the most affordable restaurant. Full desi meal (daal, sabzi, chapatti, lassi) costs PKR 150–200.
Where do students eat in Narowal?▼
Student Café near Government College is the most popular budget spot — breakfast (paratha + chai) under PKR 100. Punjab Darbar on Main Bazaar is also popular.
Are there 24-hour cheap food options in Narowal?▼
Yes. Truck Adda Dhaba on Truck Adda Road is open 24 hours serving nihari, daal, and chapatti at very affordable prices.
What is the cheapest restaurant near Narowal college?▼
Student Café near Government College Narowal is the most affordable café for students — full breakfast under PKR 100. Punjab Darbar on Main Bazaar is also within walking distance of the college area.
