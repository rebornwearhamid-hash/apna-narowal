Tips for Visiting NADRA Narowal

Arrive before 9am to get an early token. Bring originals and one photocopy of each document. Thursdays are typically less busy than Mondays and Tuesdays. For urgent cases, the executive counter has faster service at higher cost.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is NADRA Office Narowal contact number?▼
NADRA Office Narowal contact: 0542-411050. Address: Civil Lines, Near DC Office, Narowal. National helpline (free): 0800-23232, available Mon–Fri.
What are NADRA Narowal opening hours?▼
NADRA Office Narowal is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Closed weekends and public holidays.
What documents do I need for CNIC in Narowal?▼
New CNIC: B-Form, parent CNIC, birth certificate, 2 photos — all with one photocopy. Renewal: old CNIC and 2 passport photos.
Is there a NADRA office in Shakargarh?▼
Yes. NADRA Shakargarh is on Main Road near Tehsil Office. Contact: 0544-500120. Open Mon–Fri 9am–5pm.
How long does CNIC take in Narowal?▼
New CNIC or renewal takes 15–30 days. You receive an SMS when your card is ready for collection.
