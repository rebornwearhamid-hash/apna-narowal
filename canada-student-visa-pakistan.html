🇨🇦 Canada Study Permit is issued by IRCC (Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada). It allows Pakistani students to study at Designated Learning Institutions (DLIs) in Canada. Canada is one of the top destinations for Pakistani students due to its Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) and clear path to Permanent Residence.
Step by Step

How to Apply for Canada Study Permit from Pakistan

  • Research and choose a Canadian university or college. Make sure it is a Designated Learning Institution (DLI) — check the list at canada.ca. Popular choices for Pakistani students: University of Toronto, University of British Columbia, Ryerson University (Toronto Metropolitan), York University, Seneca College, Humber College, Conestoga College.
  • Apply for admission. Apply directly to the university or college website. Submit your academic transcripts, IELTS score, Statement of Purpose (SOP), and letters of recommendation. Application deadlines: January (Fall September intake) and September (Winter January intake).
  • Receive your Acceptance/Offer Letter from a DLI. This is mandatory for study permit application. Accept the offer and pay the deposit.
  • Prepare financial documents. Show CAD 10,000 (approximately PKR 20 lakh) plus your first year tuition fees in your bank account. You need 6 months of bank statements. Parents can sponsor with their own bank statements plus a sponsorship letter.
  • Write your Statement of Purpose (SOP). A strong SOP explaining why you chose Canada, your chosen field of study, career goals, and why you will return to Pakistan after studies is critical for visa approval.
  • Create an IRCC account. Go to canada.ca/ircc and create an account. Apply online for the study permit.
  • Upload all documents including acceptance letter, financial proof, IELTS, SOP, passport, photos, and any additional documents requested.
  • Pay the study permit fee of CAD 150 (approximately PKR 30,000) online via credit/debit card.
  • Biometrics. IRCC will send a biometric request letter. Book and attend biometrics at VFS Global Pakistan in Lahore or Islamabad (PKR 10,800 fee).
  • Wait for decision. Processing takes 4–12 weeks. Check ircc.canada.ca for current processing times. You will receive a Letter of Introduction if approved — this confirms your study permit will be issued at the port of entry.
  • Travel to Canada. On arrival at Canadian airport, present your Letter of Introduction and port of entry officer issues your Study Permit. Welcome to Canada!

Why Canada is Best for Pakistani Students — PR Pathway

Canada is the top choice for Pakistani students who want to eventually migrate. After completing your degree you can apply for a Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) which allows you to work in Canada for up to 3 years. After gaining work experience you can apply for Permanent Residence (PR) through Express Entry or Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP).

🍁 Canada PR Pathway for Pakistani Students: Study (2-4 years) → PGWP Work Permit (1-3 years) → Express Entry or PNP Application → Permanent Residence → Canadian Citizenship (after 3 years PR). Many Narowal families have successfully followed this path.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the requirements for Canada student visa from Pakistan?▼
Canada Study Permit requirements from Pakistan include: Acceptance letter from a DLI, IELTS 6.0–6.5, bank statement showing CAD 10,000 plus first year tuition, valid passport, Statement of Purpose (SOP), and proof of ties to Pakistan. Apply online through ircc.canada.ca.
How much bank balance is needed for Canada study permit?▼
You need CAD 10,000 (approximately PKR 20 lakh) plus your first year tuition fees. For example if tuition is CAD 15,000, you need to show CAD 25,000 total. Provide 6 months of bank statements. Parents can sponsor with their bank statements plus a sponsorship letter.
How long does Canada study permit take from Pakistan?▼
Canada Study Permit currently takes 4–12 weeks for online applications from Pakistan. Processing times vary — check the current estimate at ircc.canada.ca before applying. Apply at least 3–4 months before your course start date to allow sufficient time.
Can I work in Canada on a study permit?▼
Yes. Canada Study Permit allows you to work 20 hours per week during term time and full time during scheduled breaks. After graduation you can apply for Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) to work in Canada for 1–3 years, which is a pathway to Permanent Residence.
What is a good SOP for Canada student visa from Pakistan?▼
A strong Canada SOP should explain: why you chose Canada and this specific university, why this program aligns with your career goals, your academic background, your financial ability to fund studies, and most importantly your strong ties to Pakistan (family, property, job prospects) that will bring you back after studies. A weak SOP is the main reason for Canada visa refusals.
