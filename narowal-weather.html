🌧️ Monsoon Season in Narowal

Narowal receives its heaviest rainfall during the monsoon season — July to September. July and August are the wettest months with 120–130mm of rainfall. Flash flooding can occur in low-lying areas of Narowal city and surrounding rural areas during heavy monsoon downpours. NDMA and Punjab government issue alerts during heavy rain periods.

🔥 Summer Heat in Narowal

Narowal experiences intense summer heat from May to July. Peak temperatures regularly hit 42–46°C in June. Heat strokes are a serious concern — stay hydrated, avoid going out between 11am–4pm during June, and use fans and coolers. DHQ Hospital Narowal (0542-412345) handles heat-related emergencies.

❄️ Winter in Narowal

Winters are cold from November to February. Minimum temperatures drop to 3–8°C in December and January. Dense fog is common in December–January mornings, causing road accidents on GT Road and reducing visibility. Punjab Safe Cities Authority issues fog alerts. No snowfall in Narowal — it is in the plains of Punjab.

🌫️ Fog Advisory — Narowal

⚠️ Winter Fog Alert

Dense fog is common in Narowal from December to February, especially early mornings (4am–9am). Drive slowly on GT Road and Narowal–Shakargarh Road. Use fog lights. NDMA Fog Alert helpline: 1700. Punjab Highway Patrol: 130.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the weather in Narowal today?▼
Current weather for Narowal is shown in the live widget above, updated every 30 minutes. Narowal is currently in the monsoon season (July–September 2026) with temperatures around 35–42°C and chances of rain.
What is the hottest month in Narowal?▼
June and July are the hottest months in Narowal with temperatures regularly reaching 42–46°C. The monsoon season brings relief from late July through September with rainfall and slightly cooler temperatures.
Does it rain a lot in Narowal?▼
Narowal receives significant rainfall during the monsoon season July–September with 120–130mm in July and August alone. Flash flooding can occur in low-lying areas. Outside the monsoon season Narowal is relatively dry. Annual rainfall is approximately 500–600mm.
Does it snow in Narowal?▼
No. Narowal is located in the plains of Punjab at 238 metres elevation and does not receive snowfall. Winters are cold with minimum temperatures of 3–8°C in December–January but snow does not occur. Frost may form on very cold nights in January.
What is the best time to visit Narowal?▼
The best time to visit Narowal is October to March when temperatures are pleasant at 15–28°C. The Kartarpur Corridor is most comfortable to visit in winter. Avoid June–August due to extreme heat of 40–45°C. Monsoon season July–September is beautiful but brings flooding risk.
What is the weather in Shakargarh and Zafarwal?▼
Shakargarh and Zafarwal have nearly identical weather to Narowal as all three are in the same district. Shakargarh is slightly cooler in summer and receives slightly more rainfall due to its proximity to the Ravi river and forested areas. Use the city tabs above to switch between the three cities.
