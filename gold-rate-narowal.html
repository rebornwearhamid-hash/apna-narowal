Gold Rate in Narowal 2025

Gold rates in Narowal follow the national Sarafa Bazar rates set daily by the All Pakistan Sarafa Jewellers Association. The rates are based on the international gold price (XAU/USD) converted to Pakistani Rupees. Both 22 carat jewellery gold and 24 carat pure gold are available from local jewellers in Main Bazaar Narowal.

Where to Buy Gold in Narowal

Narowal Main Bazaar and GT Road area have several gold jewellers. Sarafa Bazar in Narowal city centre has the highest concentration of gold shops. When buying, always ask for the current day rate and get a receipt with purity stamp. Reputable shops will also test gold purity on request.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the gold rate in Narowal today?▼
Today gold rate in Narowal is shown in the live widget above. 24 carat gold rate and 22 carat gold rate are updated daily based on national Sarafa Bazar prices. The rates change every day based on international gold market and USD/PKR exchange rate.
What is 22 carat gold rate per tola in Narowal?▼
22 carat gold rate per tola in Narowal is shown in the live table above. It is calculated as 91.7% of 24 carat rate. One tola equals 11.664 grams. 22 carat is the most common purity for gold jewellery in Pakistan.
Where can I buy gold in Narowal?▼
Gold jewellery shops are in Main Bazaar Narowal, Sarafa Bazar, and GT Road area. Always check the daily Sarafa rate before purchasing and get a stamped receipt showing purity. Making charges (ujrat) are extra and vary by design.
What is the difference between 22 and 24 carat gold?▼
24 carat gold is pure gold (99.9%) used for investment — bars, coins, and bullion. 22 carat is 91.7% pure gold mixed with copper and silver, making it harder and more suitable for jewellery. Most jewellery sold in Narowal is 22 carat.
Is gold a good investment in Pakistan 2025?▼
Gold has historically been a strong store of value in Pakistan, often rising with PKR depreciation and inflation. However gold prices can be volatile. Consult a financial advisor before making large gold investments. Zakat is also payable on gold above the nisab threshold.
How are gold rates set in Pakistan?▼
Gold rates in Pakistan are set daily by the All Pakistan Sarafa Jewellers Association based on the London Bullion Market Association (LBMA) price converted to PKR using the State Bank of Pakistan exchange rate. All cities including Narowal follow the same national rate.
