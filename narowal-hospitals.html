Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How many hospitals are in Narowal?▼
Narowal district has 8 main hospitals — DHQ Hospital, Al Rehman Hospital, Medicare Hospital, Ibn Sina Hospital, City Clinic in Narowal city, plus THQ hospitals in Shakargarh and Zafarwal.
Which is the best hospital in Narowal?▼
Al Rehman Hospital on GT Road is the best rated private hospital (4.7★, 89 reviews). DHQ Hospital Narowal is the best free government hospital with all departments available 24 hours.
Are there free hospitals in Narowal?▼
Yes. DHQ Hospital Narowal, THQ Hospital Shakargarh, and THQ Hospital Zafarwal are government hospitals providing completely free healthcare including emergency, OPD, surgery, and maternity.
Which hospital is best for emergency in Narowal at night?▼
For free emergency: DHQ Hospital Narowal (0542-412345). For private emergency: Al Rehman Hospital (0542-411111). Both open 24 hours. For ambulance call Rescue 1122 (free).
Is there a hospital in Shakargarh?▼
Yes. THQ Hospital Shakargarh on Hospital Road (0544-501234) is free, open 24 hours with emergency and maternity departments.
