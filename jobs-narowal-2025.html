Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I find government jobs in Narowal?▼
Check ppsc.gop.pk for Punjab government jobs including teaching, health, and admin. Also check nts.org.pk for NTS-based recruitments and daily Jang and Dawn newspapers.
Are there teaching jobs in Narowal?▼
Yes. Punjab Education Department regularly recruits SST and EST teachers via PPSC for Narowal district schools. Private schools like The Educators and Beacon House also hire teachers directly.
How to find private jobs in Narowal?▼
Search OLX Pakistan (filter by Narowal), join Facebook groups named Jobs in Narowal, or contact local businesses and hospitals listed on Apna Narowal directly.
What types of jobs are available in Narowal?▼
Common jobs in Narowal include government teaching and health positions, private school teaching, hospital staff, retail and shop management, agricultural work, and small business roles.
