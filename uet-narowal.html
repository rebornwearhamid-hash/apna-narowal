UET Narowal Admissions 2026 — How to Apply

  • Register and appear for ECAT (Engineering College Admission Test) — conducted by UET Lahore annually. Registration opens around April-May each year.
  • Check your ECAT result and merit position at uet.edu.pk
  • During UET admission window (July-August) apply online at uet.edu.pk
  • Select UET Narowal Sub-Campus as your campus preference when filling the form
  • Upload required documents: CNIC/B-Form, Matric certificate, Intermediate certificate (Pre-Engineering or Computer Science), 2 photos
  • Pay the application fee via bank challan
  • Check merit list published on uet.edu.pk — usually released in September
  • If selected, complete enrollment by depositing semester fee at UET Narowal campus

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a UET campus in Narowal?▼
Yes. UET (University of Engineering and Technology) Lahore has a sub-campus in Narowal district offering BS Engineering programs. Students who study at UET Narowal receive a degree from UET Lahore — one of Pakistan's most prestigious engineering universities.
What programs does UET Narowal offer?▼
UET Narowal Sub-Campus offers BS Engineering programs including Electrical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Computer Engineering and Mechanical Engineering. Program availability may change each academic year — always confirm at uet.edu.pk.
How to apply for UET Narowal admissions 2026?▼
First appear for ECAT (Engineering College Admission Test) conducted by UET Lahore. Then apply online at uet.edu.pk during the admission window (typically July-August). Select UET Narowal Sub-Campus in your campus preferences. Admission is merit-based.
What is the difference between UET Narowal and UON?▼
UET Narowal is a sub-campus of UET Lahore focused exclusively on engineering (Electrical, Civil, Computer, Mechanical). University of Narowal (UON) is a separate independent university offering arts, sciences, commerce and education programs. Both are public government universities.
What is UET Narowal contact number?▼
UET Narowal Sub-Campus contact number is 0542-414500. You can also contact UET main campus at 042-99029201 or visit uet.edu.pk for all campus information. For admission queries use the UET online admission portal.
