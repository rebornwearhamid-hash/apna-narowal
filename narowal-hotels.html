Tips for Kartarpur Pilgrims — Where to Stay

If you are an Indian Sikh pilgrim visiting Kartarpur Corridor, accommodation options directly at Kartarpur are limited. The best options are:

🕌 For Sikh Pilgrims

  • → Stay in Shakargarh (15 km from Kartarpur)
  • → Shakargarh Inn recommended
  • → Free Langar at Gurdwara (no food needed)
  • → Day visit only — return same day
  • → Accommodation also near Dera Baba Nanak (India)

🇵🇰 For Pakistani Visitors

  • → Narowal city hotels for overnight
  • → Most visitors do day trip from Lahore
  • → Lahore to Kartarpur = 2 hours by car
  • → Many return same day — no hotel needed
  • → Al-Farooq Hotel best in Narowal

Nearest Hotels from Lahore for Kartarpur Visit

Most Pakistani visitors to Kartarpur do a day trip from Lahore (120 km, 2 hours) and do not need to book hotels in Narowal. However if you plan to spend more time in Narowal district or visit multiple attractions, the hotels listed above are your best options.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best hotels in Narowal?▼
Al-Farooq Hotel on GT Road (0542-413001) is the most recommended hotel in Narowal with AC rooms and a restaurant. For budget stays City Guest House near the bus stand (0542-414200) is a good option. Pearl Guest House on College Road is good for families.
Is there a hotel near Kartarpur Corridor?▼
The nearest hotels to Kartarpur Corridor are in Shakargarh city (15 km). Shakargarh Inn (0544-503500) is the best option for pilgrims. Narowal city hotels (35 km from Kartarpur) are also an option. There are limited accommodation options directly at Kartarpur.
What is the room rate in Narowal hotels?▼
Budget guest houses in Narowal charge PKR 1,500–2,500 per night. Mid-range hotels with AC charge PKR 2,500–4,500 per night. Always call ahead to confirm current rates and availability as prices may change especially during Eid and festive seasons.
Do Narowal hotels provide breakfast?▼
Most budget guest houses in Narowal do not include breakfast in the room rate. Al-Farooq Hotel has an in-house restaurant. Many local dhabas and restaurants near GT Road serve affordable breakfast from early morning.
Can I book Narowal hotels online?▼
Most Narowal hotels do not yet have online booking. Call the hotels directly to check room availability and rates. For large groups or festive season visits it is best to call and book in advance to secure a room.
