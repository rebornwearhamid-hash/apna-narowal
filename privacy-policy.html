1. Introduction

Apna Narowal ("we," "our," or "us") operates apnanarowal.pk, a free local directory and information website for Narowal district, Punjab, Pakistan. We provide information on local businesses, government services, utility rates, weather, education and other community resources for residents of Narowal, Shakargarh and Zafarwal tehsils.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Apna Narowal, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Apna Narowal is designed as a simple informational website. We collect minimal information, which may include:

  • Information you provide voluntarily — such as when you submit a business listing or contact us by email. This may include your name, business details, or message content.
  • Automatically collected technical data — such as your browser type, device type, general location (city/country level, not precise GPS), pages visited, and time spent on the site, typically gathered through standard web server logs or analytics tools.
  • Location data for weather/tools — some pages (such as the weather tool) may request or use approximate location data solely to display relevant local information. This is processed in your browser and is not stored by us.

We do not require account creation, and we do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information such as CNIC numbers, financial account details, or health records through this website.

3. How We Use Your Information

Any information collected is used only for the following purposes:

  • To respond to inquiries, business listing submissions, or messages sent via email
  • To improve website content, layout and usefulness for Narowal residents
  • To understand general visitor trends (e.g. which pages are most useful) so we can prioritize future content
  • To display accurate local information such as weather, prayer times, or currency rates based on general location

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

Apna Narowal uses the following third-party services to power certain features. These services may collect data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Open-Meteo — provides live weather data (no personal data shared, only approximate location for weather lookup)
  • ExchangeRate-API — provides live currency and gold rate conversions
  • Aladhan API — provides Islamic prayer time calculations
  • Email (Google/Gmail) — used for contact and business listing submissions; subject to Google's own privacy policy
  • Hosting provider (Netlify) — hosts our website files and may log standard technical/server data as part of normal web hosting operations

We link to official government websites (such as MTMIS Punjab, DLIMS Punjab, FBR, PTA, NADRA, Passport Office, BISP and others) for verification tools. Once you leave our site to visit these official portals, their own privacy policies apply — Apna Narowal has no control over data handled by these external government systems.

5. Cookies

Our website may use minimal cookies or local browser storage for basic functionality, such as remembering your last-used city selection on the weather page. We do not use cookies for cross-site tracking or targeted advertising. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time; this will not prevent you from using most parts of the site.

6. Business Listings & Submissions

If you submit your business, service, or organization for listing on Apna Narowal (via email), you agree that the information provided (business name, contact number, address, services offered) may be displayed publicly on the relevant page of our website. Please do not submit any information you do not wish to be publicly visible. You may request removal or correction of your listing at any time by contacting us.

📢 Note: Contact numbers, addresses and rates for government offices, hospitals, businesses and services listed on Apna Narowal are collected from publicly available sources and community submissions. While we make reasonable efforts to keep this information accurate, we recommend verifying critical information (such as fees, deadlines or official procedures) directly with the relevant office before relying on it.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect any information submitted to us. Our website is served over HTTPS (secure connection). However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of any information you share with us via email or contact forms.

8. Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal information we hold about you (such as a business listing submission)
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your business listing or submitted information
  • Opt out of any future communication from us

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 11 below.

9. Children's Privacy

Apna Narowal is a general-audience informational website intended for the residents of Narowal district. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, new features, or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your information, please reach out to us:
