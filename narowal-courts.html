Types of Legal Cases — Which Court to Go To

💔
Divorce / Khula
Family Court Narowal. Hire a family lawyer. Khula takes 90 days minimum. Contact Bar Association for advocate referral.
👶
Child Custody
Family Court Narowal. Mother gets custody of young children by default. Father can file for guardianship. Quick hearings in family court.
🏠
Property Dispute
Civil Court (built property) or Revenue Court/DC Office (agricultural land). File civil suit with property documents.
🚔
FIR / Criminal
First register FIR at Police Station. Then Magistrate Court handles bail and initial hearing. Sessions Court for serious cases.
💰
Money Recovery
Civil Court Narowal. File recovery suit with cheque, agreement or evidence of debt. Court issues decree and can order asset attachment.
🌾
Land / Mutation
Revenue Court at DC Office Narowal for agricultural land. Patwari and Tehsildar handle mutation (intiqal) of land records.

How to Find a Lawyer in Narowal

  • Visit Narowal Bar Association office at the District Court Complex, Court Road, Civil Lines, Narowal
  • Tell the Bar Association your case type — they will refer you to a suitable advocate
  • Meet 2-3 advocates before deciding — compare fees and experience
  • Ask about the advocate's experience with your specific case type (family, criminal, property etc)
  • Agree on legal fees in writing — advocates typically charge PKR 5,000–50,000 depending on case complexity
  • Provide all original documents to your advocate and keep copies yourself
  • Attend all court hearings with your advocate on the scheduled dates

How to File a Case in Narowal Court — Step by Step

  • Determine which court handles your matter — Family, Civil, Sessions or Revenue Court
  • Hire a registered advocate from Narowal Bar Association
  • Your advocate prepares the petition/plaint — the formal legal document
  • Pay the court fee via bank challan at HBL or NBP Narowal — fee varies by case type
  • File the petition at the relevant court counter with all documents and court fee receipt
  • Court issues a cause number and first date of hearing
  • Opposing party is served with summons by the court
  • Attend all hearing dates — cases typically take 6 months to several years depending on complexity
⚠️ Legal Tip: Always hire a registered advocate from Narowal Bar Association. Never pay anyone who claims to settle cases outside court without proper legal process. Always keep copies of all documents given to your lawyer.
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is District Court Narowal?▼
District Court Narowal is on Court Road, Civil Lines, Narowal — near the DC Office. Court hours are Monday to Friday 9am to 4pm. Contact: 0542-412200. All major courts (Sessions, Family, Civil, Magistrate) are in the same court complex.
How to find a lawyer in Narowal?▼
Visit Narowal Bar Association at the District Court Complex, Court Road, Narowal. The Bar Association will refer you to a registered advocate for your case type. Advocate fees in Narowal range from PKR 5,000 for simple matters to PKR 50,000+ for complex cases.
Which court handles divorce cases in Narowal?▼
Family Court Narowal handles all divorce, khula, child custody, maintenance and dower cases. The Family Court is inside the District Court complex on Court Road, Civil Lines. Khula cases take a minimum of 90 days. Hire a family law advocate from Narowal Bar Association.
How to file a property case in Narowal?▼
For built property disputes file a civil suit at Civil Court Narowal. For agricultural land disputes go to the Revenue Court at DC Office Narowal (0542-412100). Hire a civil advocate from Narowal Bar Association. Bring all property documents including registry, Fard and any agreements.
Is free legal aid available in Narowal?▼
Yes. Punjab Legal Aid Committee (PLAC) provides free legal aid to deserving persons. Call 0800-00008 for information. The District Legal Aid Office may also be contacted through the District Court Narowal. Free aid is available for poor families, women and vulnerable persons.
