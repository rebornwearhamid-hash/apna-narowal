How to Get Admission in Narowal Colleges

  • Obtain your Matric result (or equivalent) with required marks for your intended program
  • Visit the college of your choice during admission season (June-August) to collect the prospectus/admission form
  • Fill the form with CNIC/B-Form, Matric certificate/DMC, domicile certificate and passport photos
  • Submit the completed form with required documents at the college office before the deadline
  • Colleges publish a merit list based on Matric marks for competitive programs like Pre-Medical
  • If selected, pay the admission fee at the designated bank and complete enrollment
  • Attend orientation and begin classes for the new academic session

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best colleges in Narowal?▼
Government Degree College Narowal and Government College for Women Narowal are the main government colleges in Narowal city. Government Degree College Shakargarh serves Shakargarh tehsil. These offer FSc, ICS, ICOM and BA/BSc programs at affordable government fees of PKR 3,000-8,000 per year.
How to get admission in Government College Narowal?▼
Admission requires your Matric certificate with required marks (varies by program, Pre-Medical is most competitive), CNIC/B-Form, domicile certificate and passport photos. Admission forms are available at the college office typically in June-July for the new academic session. Merit is based on Matric marks.
What programs are offered at Narowal colleges?▼
Narowal colleges offer FSc Pre-Medical, FSc Pre-Engineering, ICS (Computer Science), ICOM (Commerce), FA (Arts), and BA/BSc degree programs. Government Degree College Narowal also offers various Bachelor's degree programs for students after completing intermediate.
What is the fee at government colleges in Narowal?▼
Government college fees in Narowal are very affordable, typically PKR 3,000-8,000 per year for intermediate programs, making quality education accessible. Private colleges and academies charge significantly more, ranging from PKR 30,000-100,000+ per year depending on the institution.
