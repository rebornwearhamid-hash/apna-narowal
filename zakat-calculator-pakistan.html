Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How is Zakat calculated in Pakistan?▼
Zakat in Pakistan is 2.5% of your total eligible assets (gold, cash, savings, business stock) that have been above the Nisab for one full lunar year. Use the calculator above for your exact amount.
What is the Nisab for Zakat in Pakistan 2025?▼
The silver Nisab in Pakistan for 2025 is approximately PKR 95,000 to 1,10,000. The gold Nisab is approximately PKR 13 to 15 lakh depending on current gold prices.
Is Zakat required on gold jewellery in Pakistan?▼
Yes. According to majority of Pakistani scholars, Zakat is payable on gold jewellery above the Nisab threshold at 2.5% of its current market value.
What is the gold price per gram in Pakistan 2025?▼
Gold price in Pakistan fluctuates daily. As of 2025 it is approximately PKR 17,000–19,000 per gram. Check Sarafa Bazar rates or call a local jeweller for the current price.
