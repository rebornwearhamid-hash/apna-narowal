Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the dollar rate in Pakistan today?▼
The live USD to PKR rate is shown in the exchange rate widget above. Pakistan dollar rates change daily based on the interbank forex market. The open market rate at money changers is typically 1-5 rupees different from the interbank rate.
What is the Saudi Riyal rate in Pakistan today?▼
Saudi Riyal to PKR rate is shown in the live widget above. SAR is the most searched currency rate in Pakistan as millions of Pakistani workers are employed in Saudi Arabia. The rate follows the USD/PKR rate since SAR is pegged to the USD.
Where to exchange currency in Narowal?▼
Exchange currency at HBL Bank (0542-412500), NBP Bank (0542-412550) or MCB Bank (0542-413200) in Narowal at the official interbank rate. Open market exchange (slightly different rate) is available at authorised money changers in Main Bazaar Narowal.
What is interbank vs open market rate?▼
The interbank rate is the official rate set by the State Bank of Pakistan for bank-to-bank transactions. The open market rate at money changers is usually 1-5 rupees different. For regular transactions banks give the interbank rate. For cash exchange money changers give the open market rate.
How to send money from Saudi Arabia to Narowal?▼
Use Al Rajhi Bank transfer to HBL Pakistan, Western Union, MoneyGram, or online services like Wise. The recipient in Narowal can collect at HBL Bank GT Road Narowal (0542-412500). Bank transfers give the best exchange rate. Always compare rates before sending.
