How to Register for BISP in Narowal 2026

  • Send your 13-digit CNIC (without dashes) as SMS to 8171 to check if you are already registered
  • If not registered, visit BISP Tehsil Office Narowal (Civil Lines, near DC Office) with your original CNIC
  • Fill the NSER (National Socio-Economic Registry) survey form — this determines your eligibility
  • The BISP team may also conduct a home survey to verify your household poverty score
  • If eligible you will receive an SMS on your registered mobile number
  • Collect your Benazir Kafaalat Card from the BISP office with your CNIC
  • Use the Kafaalat card at HBL or UBL bank ATM or branch to withdraw your payment
  • Check payment dates by sending CNIC to 8171 or calling 0800-26477
⚠️ Important: BISP registration is FREE. Do not pay anyone to register you for BISP. If anyone asks for money in exchange for BISP registration, report them to BISP helpline 0800-26477 immediately.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How to check BISP payment in Narowal?▼
Send your 13-digit CNIC (without dashes) as SMS to 8171. You will receive an instant reply with your payment status — free of charge. You can also call BISP helpline 0800-26477 (free, 24 hours) or visit bisp.gov.pk online.
Where is BISP office in Narowal?▼
BISP Tehsil Office Narowal is near the DC Office, Civil Lines, Narowal. Phone: 0542-412800. BISP office Shakargarh is on Main Road Shakargarh: 0544-500500. Office hours are Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm. BISP helpline 0800-26477 is available 24 hours free.
How much is BISP payment in 2026?▼
BISP Kafaalat payment in 2026 is PKR 10,500 per quarter (every 3 months) for eligible families. This is PKR 3,500 per month. Additional payments are available through Taleemi Wazaif (education stipend) and Nashonuma (nutrition) programmes.
How to register for BISP in Narowal 2026?▼
Visit BISP Tehsil Office Narowal (Civil Lines, near DC Office) with your original CNIC. Registration is through the NSER (National Socio-Economic Registry) survey which assesses household poverty. Registration is completely free — never pay anyone to register you.
Where to collect BISP payment in Narowal?▼
Collect your BISP payment at HBL Bank ATM (GT Road Narowal) or UBL Bank Narowal using your Kafaalat card. HBL Konnect mobile agents in Narowal bazaar also pay. BISP mobile camps are held in rural areas — check with BISP office for camp schedule.
My BISP payment is missing — what to do?▼
First check status by sending CNIC to 8171. If it shows payment released but not received, call BISP helpline 0800-26477 immediately. Visit BISP Narowal office with your CNIC and Kafaalat card. Do not wait — report missing payments within the same quarter.
