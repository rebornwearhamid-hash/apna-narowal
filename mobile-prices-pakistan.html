How to Check PTA Approval Status

  • Dial *#06# on your phone to get your IMEI number (15-digit code)
  • Visit dirbs.pta.gov.pk and enter your IMEI number
  • Alternative: Send SMS with your IMEI number to 8484
  • System shows if phone is PTA Approved (green), Not Registered (needs tax) or Blocked
  • If unregistered, you have 60 days to pay tax before the phone gets blocked from local networks
  • Pay tax online at dirbs.pta.gov.pk using your CNIC or Passport, then complete registration

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How to calculate PTA tax on mobile phones in Pakistan?▼
PTA tax is calculated based on the phone's value in USD and whether it is registered on Passport or CNIC. Tax ranges from PKR 1,850 for budget phones to PKR 55,000+ for premium phones. Use the calculator above for an estimate, then check exact tax at dirbs.pta.gov.pk using your phone's IMEI number.
How to check if a mobile phone is PTA approved?▼
Dial *#06# on your phone to get the IMEI number, then visit dirbs.pta.gov.pk or send SMS with your IMEI to 8484. The system shows if the phone is PTA approved, blocked, or needs tax payment for continued network access.
What happens if I don't pay PTA tax on my phone?▼
If you don't pay PTA tax within 60 days of using a foreign SIM in an unregistered phone, the phone gets blocked and won't work with any Pakistani SIM card. You must pay the tax and complete registration at dirbs.pta.gov.pk to restore network access.
Where to buy mobile phones in Narowal?▼
Mobile phones are available at the Narowal mobile market in Main Bazaar with various local dealers offering new and used phones. For guaranteed authentic products consider authorized dealers in Sialkot or Lahore, or trusted online marketplaces with PTA-approved phones.
