Net Metering — Sell Excess Power Back to LESCO/GEPCO

  • Install an on-grid or hybrid solar system from a NEPRA-approved installer with net metering capable inverter
  • Visit your local LESCO/GEPCO office with CNIC, latest electricity bill and property documents
  • Submit net metering application along with technical specifications of your solar system from the installer
  • LESCO/GEPCO team conducts a site inspection to verify system installation meets standards
  • Bidirectional meter is installed — replaces your old meter, tracks both consumption and export to grid
  • Excess solar power you generate is credited to your bill at the net metering rate — reducing your monthly electricity cost significantly
  • Processing time: 4–8 weeks from application to bidirectional meter installation

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a 5kW solar system cost in Pakistan 2026?▼
A complete 5kW on-grid solar system costs approximately PKR 750,000 to 1,100,000 including panels, inverter, mounting structure and installation. This system suits a typical Narowal home with fans, lights, fridge, one AC unit and washing machine. Hybrid systems with battery backup cost 40-60% more.
What is net metering and how to apply in Punjab?▼
Net metering lets you sell excess solar electricity back to LESCO/GEPCO grid, reducing your bills. Apply at your local LESCO/GEPCO office with CNIC, electricity bill, property documents and technical specs from your NEPRA-approved installer. A bidirectional meter is installed after inspection. Processing takes 4-8 weeks.
How many solar panels do I need for my home in Narowal?▼
For an average Narowal home using 500-800 units per month, a 5kW system with 10-12 panels of 450-550 watts each is typically sufficient. Homes with AC units and heavy appliances may need a 8-10kW system with 16-20 panels. Use the calculator above for a personalized estimate.
Is solar worth it in Narowal with frequent load shedding?▼
Yes. Solar systems typically pay for themselves within 3-5 years through reduced electricity bills. A hybrid system with battery backup is especially valuable in Narowal — it provides power during load shedding hours while also reducing your monthly bill through solar generation during the day.
What is the difference between on-grid and hybrid solar systems?▼
On-grid systems have no battery and shut off during load shedding — best for reducing bills via net metering where load shedding isn't a big issue. Hybrid systems include battery backup so they continue supplying power during outages — recommended for Narowal due to frequent unscheduled load shedding. Off-grid systems are fully independent of the grid, common in rural areas without grid connection.
