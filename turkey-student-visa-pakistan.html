🇹🇷 Turkey (Türkiye) is increasingly popular among Pakistani students — shared Islamic culture, affordable living, English-taught programs, and strong scholarship opportunities make it an attractive destination. Islamabad to Istanbul flights are direct and frequent. Pakistani community in Turkey is well-established especially in Istanbul, Ankara and Bursa.
Step by Step

How to Apply for Turkey Student Visa from Pakistan

  • Choose your path — Scholarship or Self-funded. Option A: Apply for Turkiye Burslari scholarship (Jan–Feb deadline, full funding). Option B: Apply directly to Turkish university (rolling admissions, you pay fees). For most Pakistani students Turkiye Burslari is the first choice.
  • Apply for Turkiye Burslari (if applying for scholarship). Go to turkiyeburslari.gov.tr. Create an account, select up to 12 universities and programs. Upload: academic transcripts (70%+ for undergraduate, 75%+ for graduate), motivation letter, CV, language certificate (IELTS or Turkish), and two recommendation letters. Deadline is typically February 20 each year. Results announced July–August.
  • Apply directly to Turkish university (self-funded path). Visit the university website or use YÖK Atlas (yokatlas.yok.gov.tr) to find programs. Many Turkish state universities accept Pakistani students directly based on FSc/A-Level marks without YOS exam. Submit online application with academic documents and IELTS (for English programs).
  • YOS exam (if required by university). Some Turkish universities require YOS (Yabanci Ogrenci Sinavi) — a math and IQ-based entrance exam for international students. Check if your target university requires YOS. YOS is held annually — preparation books and courses are available online.
  • Receive your acceptance letter. After successful application you receive an admission letter from the Turkish university. This is required for your student visa application.
  • Apply for Turkey Student Visa at Turkish Embassy Islamabad. Go to islamabad.emb.mfa.gov.tr and book a visa appointment. Turkish Embassy is in Islamabad (Diplomatic Enclave). Narowal students travel to Islamabad (~4.5 hours via Lahore). Turkish Consulate Karachi is also an option.
  • Attend visa appointment and submit documents. Bring all original documents. Pay the visa fee. Processing takes 2–4 weeks. You will receive a student visa (D-type) for Turkey.
  • Arrive in Turkey and get residence permit. On arrival register at the local Yabancılar Şubesi (foreigners police office) within 30 days to get your student residence permit (Öğrenci İkamet İzni). Your university international office will assist you.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How to apply for Turkiye Burslari scholarship from Pakistan?▼
Go to turkiyeburslari.gov.tr between January and February. Create an account, select up to 12 universities and programs, upload FSc/A-Level transcripts (70%+ marks for undergrad), IELTS or Turkish certificate, motivation letter, CV, and two recommendation letters. Results are announced in July–August. The scholarship covers tuition, accommodation, monthly stipend TRY 5,000–7,000, health insurance and one return flight.
Is Turkey safe and good for Pakistani students?▼
Yes. Turkey is one of the most welcoming countries for Pakistani students. Shared Islamic culture means halal food is everywhere, mosques are abundant, and Pakistani students feel at home. Pakistani community is well established in Istanbul, Ankara and Bursa. Turkey and Pakistan have excellent diplomatic relations and Pakistanis get strong support from Turkish authorities.
Do I need Turkish language for Turkey from Pakistan?▼
For Turkish-taught undergraduate programs: yes, you need TÖMER B2 certificate or you complete a 1-year Turkish preparatory program (Hazırlık). For English-taught programs: IELTS 6.0+ or TOEFL 72+ is required. Turkiye Burslari scholarship includes a free 1-year Turkish language course before your main program begins — so no prior Turkish knowledge needed.
What is the YOS exam for Turkey?▼
YOS (Yabanci Ogrenci Sinavi) is a university entrance exam for international students at some Turkish universities. It tests basic mathematics and IQ/abstract reasoning. Not all universities require it — many accept Pakistani students based on FSc or A-Level grades alone. Check your target university's international student admission requirements. YOS preparation materials are available online in Urdu.
How much does it cost to live in Turkey as a Pakistani student?▼
Living costs in Turkey are approximately USD 400–700 per month covering food, transport, phone and personal expenses. Istanbul is more expensive than Ankara or Bursa. With Turkiye Burslari scholarship the monthly stipend of TRY 5,000–7,000 comfortably covers living costs. Without scholarship total costs are much lower than UK, Canada or Australia.
