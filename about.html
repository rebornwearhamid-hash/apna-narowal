About Apna Narowal

A free, community-focused local directory built for the people of Narowal district — اپنا نارووال، اپنے لوگوں کے لیے۔

Who We Are

Apna Narowal is a community-run local information portal serving Narowal district, Punjab, Pakistan — covering the three tehsils of Narowal, Shakargarh, and Zafarwal. We are not a government body or a commercial venture; we are a free public resource built to make everyday life easier for local residents, students, job seekers, and anyone with ties to the district.

Why We Started

Finding reliable local information about Narowal district online used to mean scattered Facebook posts, outdated phone numbers, or asking around. There was no single place bringing together government office contacts, hospital timings, school listings, weather, utility rates, study-abroad guidance, and everyday services — all specific to Narowal, Shakargarh and Zafarwal.

Apna Narowal exists to close that gap: one accurate, easy-to-navigate resource for anyone who lives in, works in, or has family in Narowal district — whether you're checking today's gold rate, looking for the nearest blood bank, applying for a passport, or exploring a study visa for your child.

Our Approach

Everything on Apna Narowal is offered completely free of charge. Information is compiled from publicly available sources, official government portals, and community input. We link directly to official verification tools (like MTMIS Punjab, DLIMS, FBR, and PTA) wherever possible, so residents can always confirm critical details at the source.

We are an independent, non-commercial project. We do not charge for listings or information, and we're not affiliated with the Government of Punjab or the District Administration of Narowal — though we link to their official resources throughout the site.
