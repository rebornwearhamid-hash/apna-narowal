🇦🇺 Australia Student Visa Subclass 500 allows Pakistani students to study full-time at a CRICOS-registered Australian institution. The key requirement unique to Australia is the GTE (Genuine Temporary Entrant) statement — proving you intend to study temporarily and return to Pakistan. You can work 48 hours per fortnight during term time.
Step by Step

How to Apply for Australia Student Visa from Pakistan

  • Choose a CRICOS-registered institution. All Australian universities and colleges must be registered on CRICOS (Commonwealth Register of Institutions and Courses for Overseas Students). Check at cricos.teqsa.gov.au. Top choices for Pakistani students: University of Melbourne, University of Sydney, RMIT University, Griffith University, University of Southern Queensland, Central Queensland University (CQU).
  • Apply for admission and receive your Offer Letter. Apply directly to the institution. Submit FSc/A-Level certificates, IELTS score, academic transcripts and SOP. Accept the offer and pay the deposit.
  • Receive your CoE (Confirmation of Enrolment). After paying the deposit, the institution sends your CoE — a unique electronic document confirming your enrolment. This is mandatory for the visa application.
  • Purchase Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC). All international students in Australia must have OSHC for the full duration of their course. Buy from approved providers: Medibank, Bupa, nib, Allianz. Cost: AUD 600–900 per year.
  • Write your GTE (Genuine Temporary Entrant) statement. This is the most important document. Explain your genuine intention to study in Australia temporarily, your strong ties to Pakistan (family, property, career prospects), why you chose this course and institution, and why you will return to Pakistan after studies. A poor GTE leads to visa refusal.
  • Prepare financial documents. Show AUD 21,041 per year for living expenses plus your full tuition fees. Also show AUD 7,362 per year for a dependent spouse and AUD 3,152 per child if applicable. Show 3–6 months of bank statements.
  • Create an ImmiAccount. Go to immi.homeaffairs.gov.au and create an ImmiAccount. Apply online for Student Visa Subclass 500.
  • Upload all documents — CoE, OSHC, GTE statement, financial evidence, IELTS, passport, academic certificates, photos, and health examination results if requested.
  • Pay the visa application fee of AUD 710 (approximately PKR 130,000) online.
  • Health examination. You may be asked to undergo a medical examination at a Panel Physician approved by Australian authorities in Pakistan. Exam clinics are available in Lahore and Karachi.
  • Wait for decision. Processing takes 4–6 weeks if documents are complete. You will receive the visa grant notice by email with your visa label — no stamp required, it is electronic.
  • Travel to Australia. You can arrive up to 90 days before your course starts. The visa is linked to your passport electronically.

The GTE Statement — Most Important Part

⚠️ GTE (Genuine Temporary Entrant) is the #1 reason for Australia student visa refusals from Pakistan. Your GTE must convincingly explain:
  • → Your genuine intention to study — not migrate
  • → Strong ties to Pakistan (family, property, job prospects after graduation)
  • → Why you chose this specific course and institution
  • → Your career goals and how this study helps in Pakistan
  • → Your immigration history (if any previous visa refusals)
A strong, specific GTE is critical. Generic statements are rejected.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is GTE statement for Australia student visa?▼
GTE (Genuine Temporary Entrant) is a written statement explaining that you genuinely intend to study in Australia temporarily and return to Pakistan. It must cover why you chose Australia and this course, your career goals, your strong ties to Pakistan, and your immigration history. It is the most critical document and a weak GTE is the main reason for refusals from Pakistan.
How much bank balance is needed for Australia student visa?▼
You need AUD 21,041 per year for living expenses plus your full first year tuition fees. For example if tuition is AUD 25,000, you need AUD 46,041 (~PKR 84 lakh) in total. Show 3–6 months of bank statements. Parents can sponsor with their bank statements plus a sponsorship letter.
What is OSHC and is it mandatory for Australia?▼
OSHC (Overseas Student Health Cover) is mandatory health insurance for all international students in Australia. You must purchase it before applying for your visa and maintain it for your full course duration. Approved providers include Medibank, Bupa, nib and Allianz. Cost is AUD 600–900 per year.
Can I work in Australia on a student visa from Pakistan?▼
Yes. Australia Student Visa allows 48 hours of work per fortnight (2 weeks) during term time and unlimited hours during scheduled course breaks. Australian minimum wage is AUD 23.23 per hour (2026) which helps significantly offset living costs.
How to apply for Australia student visa online from Pakistan?▼
Create an ImmiAccount at immi.homeaffairs.gov.au, fill the Subclass 500 application form, upload all documents (CoE, OSHC, GTE, financial evidence, IELTS, passport), and pay AUD 710 online. No visit to an embassy is required — the entire process is online. The visa grant is sent by email.
