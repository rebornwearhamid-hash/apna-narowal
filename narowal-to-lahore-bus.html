Daewoo / Faisal Movers Booking — Online

For AC coaches with guaranteed seats, book online in advance especially for Eid and holidays. Daewoo and Faisal Movers both have apps and websites for online booking. Walk-in booking also available at the bus stand counter.

⚠️ Note: Bus and wagon timings and fares can change without notice. Always confirm with the bus stand or coach service before travelling. Fares may increase during Eid and peak travel seasons.
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the bus fare from Narowal to Lahore?▼
Narowal to Lahore fare is PKR 400–500 for local wagons, PKR 500–700 for standard coaches, and PKR 900–1,200 for AC coaches like Daewoo or Faisal Movers. Fares may be higher during Eid and holidays.
What time does the first bus leave Narowal for Lahore?▼
The first Faisal Movers coach departs at 5:30 AM and Daewoo Express at 6:00 AM. Local wagons start from around 5:30 AM and run until 9:00 PM from Narowal Bus Stand on GT Road.
How long does it take from Narowal to Lahore by bus?▼
The journey takes approximately 2.5 to 3 hours by AC coach and 3 to 3.5 hours by local wagon. Daewoo Express can do it in about 2 hours via the motorway. Traffic in Lahore can add extra time.
Where is Narowal Bus Stand?▼
Narowal Bus Stand is on GT Road, Narowal City. It is the main departure point for all intercity buses and wagons. You can reach it by rickshaw (PKR 50–100) from anywhere in Narowal city.
Is there a direct bus from Narowal to Islamabad?▼
Yes. Daewoo Express and Faisal Movers have Islamabad services from Narowal. The journey takes approximately 4–5 hours and costs PKR 1,500–2,500 for AC coach. Book at the bus stand counter or online.
What is the fare from Narowal to Sialkot?▼
Narowal to Sialkot local wagon fare is PKR 150–200 and takes about 45 minutes. Wagons depart very frequently from Narowal Bus Stand — every 15 to 20 minutes throughout the day.
