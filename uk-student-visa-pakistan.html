🇬🇧 UK Student Visa (formerly Tier 4) allows Pakistani students to study at UK universities for courses lasting more than 6 months. You need a CAS number from your university, IELTS 6.0+, and proof of funds. You can work 20 hours per week during term time.
Step by Step

How to Apply for UK Student Visa from Pakistan

  • Choose a UK university and course. Apply through UCAS (ucas.com) for undergraduate or directly to university for postgraduate. Check if the university is UKVI-approved (Student Route sponsor). Top choices for Pakistani students: University of Manchester, University of Leeds, University of Birmingham, Brunel University London.
  • Get your offer letter. Once accepted, the university sends you an unconditional offer letter. Accept the offer and pay the deposit if required.
  • Receive your CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies) number. The university sends your unique CAS number. This is mandatory for the visa application. Apply no earlier than 3 months before your course starts.
  • Take IELTS Academic test. Most UK universities require IELTS Academic 6.0–6.5 for undergraduate and 6.5–7.0 for postgraduate. Book at British Council or IDP Pakistan. IELTS centres in Lahore, Sialkot and Gujranwala are nearest to Narowal students.
  • Prepare your financial documents. Show bank statement with sufficient funds held for 28+ consecutive days. Required: GBP 1,334/month for London studies or GBP 1,023/month outside London. For a 9-month course in London: approximately GBP 12,006 (PKR 43 lakh+).
  • Apply online. Go to gov.uk/student-visa. Fill the online application form, pay the visa fee of GBP 490 and the Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS — approximately GBP 776 per year of study).
  • Book VFS appointment. After completing online application, book a biometric appointment at VFS Global Pakistan in Lahore (nearest for Narowal students). Book early — slots fill up fast.
  • Attend VFS appointment. Go to VFS Global Lahore on your appointment date with all original documents, passports, and application printout. Biometric data (fingerprints and photo) will be taken.
  • Wait for decision. Standard processing: 3–4 weeks. Priority service: 5 business days (extra fee). You can track your application online.
  • Receive visa and travel. If approved your passport will be returned with the visa vignette. Book your flights and travel to the UK. You can arrive 1 month before your course starts.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What IELTS score do I need for UK student visa?▼
Most UK universities require IELTS Academic 6.0–6.5 for undergraduate and 6.5–7.0 for postgraduate. The UK visa itself requires minimum IELTS 4.0 but your university will set a higher requirement. PTE and TOEFL are also accepted by many UK universities.
How much bank balance is needed for UK student visa from Pakistan?▼
For London studies you need GBP 1,334 per month for up to 9 months (GBP 12,006 total). For outside London it is GBP 1,023 per month (GBP 9,207 for 9 months). These funds must be in your account for 28 consecutive days before applying. Parents or sponsors can provide the funds with a supporting letter.
Where to apply for UK student visa in Pakistan near Narowal?▼
Apply online at gov.uk/student-visa then book a biometric appointment at VFS Global Pakistan in Lahore — the nearest visa application centre for Narowal students. From Narowal take a bus to Lahore (2.5 hours) for the VFS appointment. Slots fill up fast so book early.
Can I work in UK on a student visa from Pakistan?▼
Yes. UK Student Visa allows you to work 20 hours per week during term time and full time during holidays. This helps offset living costs. Many Pakistani students in the UK work part-time in restaurants, supermarkets, petrol stations and other sectors.
What is a CAS number for UK student visa?▼
CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies) is a unique reference number issued by your UK university after you accept their offer. It is mandatory for your UK Student Visa application. The CAS contains your course details, fees and university information. It is valid for 6 months from issue date.
