Step by Step — New Application

How to Apply for New Passport from Narowal

  • Apply online at passport.gov.pk. Create an account using your CNIC. Fill the passport application form online — personal details, address (Narowal), emergency contact. Select your passport type (normal/urgent) and number of pages (36 or 72). Save your application reference number.
  • Pay the passport fee. Pay at any Allied Bank (ABL) or MCB Bank branch in Narowal using your application reference number. You can also pay online via credit/debit card or Jazz Cash on the passport portal. Keep the fee payment receipt — mandatory at the passport office.
  • Book appointment at Sialkot Passport Office. After fee payment, book your appointment online at passport.gov.pk/appointment or call Sialkot office (052-9250356). Select your preferred date and time. Print or save your appointment slip on your phone.
  • Travel to Sialkot Passport Office. Sialkot is 35 km from Narowal — take a bus from Narowal Bus Stand to Sialkot Morr/GT Road (PKR 80–120, 45 minutes). Rickshaw/taxi from Sialkot bus stand to Passport Office Kutchery Road: PKR 150–200.
  • Attend appointment at Sialkot Passport Office. Bring ALL original documents. Arrive 15 minutes early. Staff verify documents, take biometrics (fingerprints + digital photo) and collect your application. No physical form — everything is digital.
  • Track your passport status. SMS your CNIC to 8400, track online at passport.gov.pk/track or call 051-111-600-100. Passport delivered by TCS courier to your address OR collect from Sialkot office.
  • Receive passport. Normal: 30 working days. Urgent: 7 working days. Emergency: same day. TCS courier delivers to your Narowal address — stay available on your registered phone number.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the passport office for Narowal residents?▼
Narowal district residents apply at Regional Passport Office Sialkot — Kutchery Road, Civil Lines, Sialkot. Phone: 052-9250356. Sialkot is 35 km from Narowal — 40–50 minutes by bus (PKR 80–120). Book appointment online at passport.gov.pk before travelling.
How to track passport status from Narowal?▼
Track passport online at passport.gov.pk/track using your CNIC or application number. Or SMS your CNIC to 8400 for instant status — no internet needed. You can also call the National Passport helpline 051-111-600-100 or Sialkot office directly at 052-9250356.
What is the fastest way to get passport from Narowal?▼
Emergency passport is the fastest — same day or within 24 hours for PKR 8,500 (36-page) or PKR 12,000 (72-page). For emergency passport you must visit Sialkot Passport Office directly with valid reason (medical emergency, urgent travel). Urgent passport (7 working days) costs PKR 5,000–8,500 and is the most practical fast option.
Can I apply for child passport from Narowal?▼
Yes. Child passport (under 18) costs PKR 1,500 for 36-page. You need the child's original NADRA B-Form, both parents' original CNICs, and both parents must be present at the Sialkot Passport Office appointment. If one parent cannot attend, a notarized consent letter is required. Apply online at passport.gov.pk and book Sialkot appointment.
How long does passport renewal take from Narowal?▼
Normal renewal: 30 working days (approximately 6 weeks). Urgent renewal: 7 working days. Emergency renewal: same day at PKR 8,500–12,000. After renewal passport is delivered by TCS courier to your Narowal address — no need to go back to Sialkot. Track via SMS 8400 or passport.gov.pk/track.
